Capture x8 aspect ratio repair

The 60.48 x 270 um NR8 route failed GRT with 9695 overflow, dominated by M3. Its 3296 root storage/shadow crossing bits, 128 status bits and 40 pointer/state bits exceed the narrow face capacity. The successor measures 123.12 x 136.08 um and preserves the NR8 RTL, two clocks, transaction latency and 68 parent entries.

The cut screen includes native M3/M5 track pitches and the actual paired 0.12 um M5 PG stripes every 5.4 um: 3464 bits / 5871 usable tracks = 0.590. Two side channels carry 832 upper outputs, 416 lower inputs and 64 status wires: 1312 bits / 2368 usable tracks = 0.554. These are capacity screens. Routed congestion and timing remain unproven. Child origins align to 0.576 um, every child gap is at least 20 um, and the parent remains 1728 x 561.6 um. The successor requires fresh assigned-pin CTS/GRT and per-pair reach derived from its exported view.

The narrow IOP probe shows that PPL snapped M3 centre 5.004 to 5.013 but retained the requested 0.024 um width. The new die assignment uses native M3 centres and 0.018 um width.
