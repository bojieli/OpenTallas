## Why lpk4 (structural redesign after the lpk3 gate2 FAIL, ep8 ref_gate/s81lk_serdes_col_lp-20261019-172317)
- ck_tx was ONE clock region over the whole TX lane (tx face reg + encoder at the N face, two relay rows, landing ring) AND the
  rx/st output side: 4855 sinks over ~1.3 mm, TT insertion 0.92-1.75 ns (boundary 1517-1709), pin spread 165 ps, setup -622 ps
  on relay row u_txr1; every reset-root hop out of that 1.5 ns region failed setup (-126..-391 ps); GRT hold repair ran 3.5 h.
- lpk4: the TX lane is a straight E column of 4 clk regions, each hop a D4/E2 landing (2-cycle wire, <= ~470 um):
  `tx` (u_txf, u_live_t, u_enc; encoder moved from W to E top at 259.2,3585.6 directly under the tx pins) -> `r0` (u_txr0,
  ck_r0 E face y 3240) -> `r1` (u_txr1, ck_r1 E y 2770) -> `ptx` (PMA TX u_ptx, ck_ptx E y 2300).
  The RX output side is its own clk region `rxo` (u_rm read side, u_sts clk side, 2-stage rx/st out regs, ck_rxo W y 3600);
  rx/st pins moved over the W column (x 45-221) so the RX chain runs straight up the W column (PMA RX -> syn -> dly -> chn ->
  out -> rm -> rx) and the TX chain straight down the E column.  rck regions syn/dly/chn/out unchanged.
- Cost (static, lp4 record `compiler`): TX +9 edges (lpk3 +3), rx/st +8 edges (lpk3 +7); flops landing 18,241 (lpk3 12,734),
  out regs 1,028 (514).  ~+5.8 ns per crossing at 1.2 GHz; owner 10-18 accepts landing/latency cost for closure.

2. Reach: PASS by construction. Every TX hop is a 2-cycle landing <= ~470 um; rx 2 out stages over ~500 um; decoder
   landings between abutting tiles; reset runs sized by lp at 405 um/hop.
3. Fanout: PASS. Every child reset pin has its own pad; live flags drive one pin; one landing per reader region.
4. Depth: PASS. Parent glue is registers only; no combinational in -> out path.
5. Storage: WAIVED (unchanged from lpk3): u_rm 8 x 512 elastic FIFO is a flop array (same RTL as the closed s81lk_rate_match

6. Floorplan: PMAs (43.2,43.2) W / (345.6,43.2) E, six children on the 43.2 lattice (enc now 259.2,3585.6); pin densities
   tx 3.0, rx 3.0, pma 4.35 pins/um (<= 6); util measured by the gate. Aspect 7.4:1 WAIVED: approved die-edge SerDes slot
   (column of 9 local clock regions, each <= ~0.5 mm).
7. Clock: fixed. 9 regional roots (clk: tx, r0, r1, ptx, rxo; pma_rck: syn, dly, chn, out), each port beside its region,
   no region spans more than ~0.6 mm; landed crossings only; pin-flop spread <= 80 ps TT+FF judged by the gate;
   io_hold_margin 0.03.
8. Schedule: PASS. Static; every added edge in the lp4 record compiler block; the only elastic element is the rate
   matcher at the off-die SerDes boundary.
