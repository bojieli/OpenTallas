## Safe-design checklist
1 Pins: inputs land directly in replica flops (control), lane/bank pins or per-lane copies; region crossings are landed in the parent (09:05 rule: pin regs only at crossings). reset: one landed run from the parent (t equal for all 17 sinks).
2 Reach: tile 1235 wide; glue sits in the channels beside its lane/bank pair; gate wire_reach decides.
3 Fanout: pos/rpos 4 copies, fw/dwn 5 copies per lane on pins, vm_we byte selects in kept AND3, RF select 4 kept copies; census at gate.
4 Depth: unchanged glue logic (<=8 levels), no in->out combinational path besides lane->bank write mux (same as lg).
5 Storage: banks are SRAM children; ctl replicas are pipeline flops, not indexed arrays.
6 Floorplan: children on the 43.2 lattice; one std-cell area in channels; aspect 1.63.
7 Clock: single root per tile (region = one clock region, 80 ps rule inside); KIND0 unloaded clk pins (insert_0..3, stage_4) open in synthesis.
8 Schedule: no latency change vs rgf_lp (lockstep bench tb_hbm_su_pgslice_rg HREG=1).
