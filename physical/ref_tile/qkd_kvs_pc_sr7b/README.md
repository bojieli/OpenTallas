# qkd_kvs_pc_sr7b -- KV-PC4 structural redesign of pc_sr6 after its GRT overflow (CRITICAL family: NO REVIEW.json / submit)

Generated by tools/qwen_kv_die/kvpc_sram_kit.py --tag sr7b --variant d + tools/ref_tile/ref_tile.py gen.  Same RTL,
pins, SDC and boundary_tcl as qkd_kvs_pc_sr6 (claude/kv-pc3-1019 @b26999d6d RTL; bench receipts kvpc_sram_sr6_1019).
Why: gate qkd_kvs_pc_sr6-20261019-180008 (ot-epyc8) GRT overflow 1028 (974 congested gcells, all over / beside bank
column A, worst at its stack ends and gaps; pc_h_sr6, same floorplan with less logic, routes with 0).  The bank's read
side (rd_out 64 b, r/w addr, ce, rclk, wclk) is on its LEF W face, so in column A (R0) every read word (return
read_data 288 b, front out_q, tq) left toward the W wall and had to cross the stack to reach ctrl / obs in the centre;
M2/M4 are blocked over the banks (M5 cap), so the words detoured through the 23 um stack gaps.  Column B (CDC, R0)
reads westward into the centre and has no congestion.  Fix: column A banks (front 5, return 5, tag queue 2) mirrored and moved in to x=86.4 (86 um W PHY region, 54 um centre channel),
orient MY: read faces look at the centre logic, write faces at the W PHY side.  One failed route = structural redesign.
pc_sr6 CTS (for reference): SS setup -300 sync / -253 after repair, FF hold +28; spread ck 69.7 TT / 67.9 FF, hck 55.3 / 51.8.
