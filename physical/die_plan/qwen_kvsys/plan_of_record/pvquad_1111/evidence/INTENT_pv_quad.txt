0. WHY LEGAL MOVES FAIL WHILE THE IDEAL BISECTION IS 0.44
   - The bisection prices a whole-type cut on M2-M7 everywhere. The binding cut is local: in the 4-row stack
     (far | near | join | near | far) two of the four ts partners of every join sit in FAR slots. Their 1024-b ts bus must
     cross the NEAR pair. Over a kv3_pv_pair the LEF blocks M1-M5 solid, and its own M6/M7 wiring leaves 21 % of the M6
     and 68 % of the M7 through-tracks. So the crossing has M7 only, in a 380-um column, and no H (M6) to jog to the
     join's W/E faces.
   - GRT maps (skeleton probes, 2 congestion iterations, the same for every arm; heat_*.png): pv_v2_i2 1,638,563, of
     which M6 1.19M and M7 0.33M. The overflow sits over the 8 NEAR pairs, V direction, ts nets (rpt: 15.2k of 18.2k units).
     pv_v4n_i2 3,217,138: the same plus the N-family far slots whose pin faces point away from the join.
   - Every assignment of 4 partners to the 4 slots of a column keeps 2 far partners, so no position/orient move inside the
     plan slots removes the crossing. Street_H is already 0.81 after pv_S; the street is not the binding cut.

2. HAND LAYOUT (rules)
   R1. Each group is a 2x2 quad around its join: two pair rows of 2, with 43.2-um streets and the join (172.8) between
       them. Every pair's pin face (one long face, M5) faces the join street. No ts bus crosses a pair.
   R2. Pinwheel: pK sits at the corner nearest join face tsK (R0 image: p0 LL->S, p1 UL->W, p2 UR->N, p3 LR->E), so the
       four 1024-b buses turn the same way and never cross each other.
   R3. Per group, the quad image (R0/MX/MY/R180) equals the base join orient. The join orient is unchanged (no M3
       0.018-um lattice issue), and the pair x-mirror class equals the join class (bit-order rule A).
   R4. Quads at the 4 corners. CHOSEN FORM "quadw": column gap 103.68 inside each quad (the join straddles it), join x
       345.6 in the quad frame, quads at g0 (8.64,43.2), g1 (1278.72,43.2), g2 (8.64,734.4), g3 (1278.72,734.4). Quad
       864.0 x 639.36, all origins on the 8.64 lattice, tops at 1373.76 (below the ft0/ft26 1056-b band at y 1410).
       Spine 873-1279 = 406 um. The narrow form "quad" (gap 25.92, the GRT-probed one) is kept as fallback
       (delta_kv_pv_quad.json).
       route_supply pv_v2 (explorer --supply): base worst p99 street_H 1.58 / core_H 1.26 -> quad ring_H 1.343,
       core_H 1.319, street_H 1.156 -> quadw ring_H 1.291, core_H 1.187, street_H 1.099.
   R5. The centre column x 794.88-1365.12 (570 um, full height) is macro-free. The die clock tap (1080,760.32) and the
       reset tap are already there. It carries the broadcast spine: pd regs and the re-sited face-bus station chains,
       entering each quad by its join street.
   R6. Core clock taps move rigidly with their core (in-core island travels with the slot): 20 taps per type.
   SVG/PNG: pv_redesign_options.{svg,png} panel C (drawn in the R0 image; the N family uses the MX image in every group).

   c) reset local taps at (8.64,544.32) and (2047.68,544.32) now fall inside pairs. The die-owner re-derive moves them
      into the quad streets.

   i_astk_W 256 b + i_vport_W_N_vo 800 b broadcast routed over the middle pair rows. The skeleton has no pd/station
   cells, so R5 (spine) is the fix. That fix is not yet measured; one probe on the re-sited plan confirms it.
   S2 GATE: route_supply is still > 1.0 (quadw 1.29), so it is NOT launchable as is. The residual is the face buses:
   i_vport_E/W_N_vo (397-800 b) and i_astk_E/W (256 b) broadcast to all 16 pairs, plus the N landing ring.
   Under owner 11-11 (a >= 512-b bus driving a cut > 0.8 gets an architecture change), i_vport needs a die-owner
   decision: one broadcast register stage per quad in the spine (pin cycle) or a 2:1 serialised vport leg. That is
   the second half of this REDESIGN.
