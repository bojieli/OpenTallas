   with the right tiles mirrored (MY) their data pins sat on the outer E channel, so the quad's inner links crossed one
   or two 458-um macros and L1 crossed two (a crossing forced onto a fabric hop: the -824 ps class).  Facing every
   data edge that has no face bus toward the middle channel makes the chain local (cbn_q11 NK bit-mm 2372 -> 1431,
   explorer 4320 -> 3379) and leaves at most one crossing per link, on an NK-input hop.  Pins stay on track (M4, x
   mirror), positions, buses, lanes, latency and reset depth are unchanged.
