# J6 SerDes t16 a3 START reach

The exact E2 attempt completed placement and [formal geometry before the route admission](s81-serdes-t16-j6-a3-auto-geom.png). I reviewed the clock and density overlay. Tight electronics under the two north PMA macros and narrow shore-side landing clusters leave most of the wide middle rectangle sparse. Formal geometry has zero diagnostic GRT overflow, horizontal demand 0.976, 812 captures over 405 µm, 100 collapsed relay chains and peak tap load 3,949. Route admission later exited RC78 and did not execute route. Keep the unchanged route held.

The west `u_cland_fstart_ft0_o` flop sits about 6 µm from its FSTART output pin. Its synchronous reset spans 3,168.558 µm from east `rst_n_t33`. The exact wrapper uses 13 pure posedge delays from the same reset input register for both `rst_n_t33` and west `rst_n_t41`. The latter sits 76.896 µm from the west capture. Moving the west relay reset to this already-local signal would preserve its sampled reset cycle. The existing east reset is still used by the east origin and the east face flops.

The west face receives data 3,016.332 µm from origin s1 and 3,019.086 µm from s2. With the fixed west and two east face capture sites, any shared origin register needs at least one data leg of 1,718.280 µm. The existing single TSTART input on the east sits 3,324.653 µm from the west capture. Even with a 150 µm first pin leg and a generous four legs of 405 µm, its 1,770 µm allowance cannot cover that span. The rising/PAD0 mode uses only two registered legs after the first pin capture, and needs at least six additional legs at the existing pin and capture sites. This geometry rules out a same-cycle move or clone driven solely by the existing east TSTART port.

I compared the exact origin and face modules using a west origin clone with the west reset and a cycle-aligned west TSTART. All four strap modes matched over 972 comparisons, and a one-cycle-late west TSTART produced 317 mismatches across the modes. This bounds the local change for a future west TSTART branch. The parent needs exact cycle-aligned delivery and a reviewed plan, or a separately proved change to START timing. No replacement wrapper or route was submitted.
