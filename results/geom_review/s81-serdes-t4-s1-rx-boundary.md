# t4 S1 SerDes receive boundary

I matched all 544 PMA q pins from the private t4 J6 kit's `macros/ot_s81_pma_rxm.lef` to the actual dec_j6c `6_final.def` p_d pins. The PMA origin (319.272, 952.56) µm and MX orientation are in the J6 kit's `macro_place.tcl`. The candidate decoder origin (1075.68, 388.8) µm and R0 are in R6 `rows/layout_t4.json`. The measured pin mapping and proposed stage sites are shown in [the reviewed figure](s81-serdes-t4-s1-rx-boundary.png).

The current plan sends the recovered data via one shell falling-edge register to the decoder's rising-edge face register. End-to-end median Manhattan distance is 648.978 µm, with a 1,304.262 µm maximum and 415 of 544 bits above 400 µm. The longest bit requires at least one half-cycle leg of 652.131 µm even with ideal intermediate placement. The best lateral translation alone still leaves 946.008 µm worst horizontal span. This S1 form fails the geometry review. Hold its route and exclude it from J7 adoption.

A candidate cut puts the existing rising-edge face capture, AM partial computation and first three full-cycle RCK transport stages in the shell. The fourth full-cycle register remains the decoder's registered input. Keep the reset-calendar transport split three plus one. The same pin positions admit a preview with half-cycle maxima 90 µm each and full-cycle maxima 281.066 µm each. The preview clears the 8.64 µm macro halo for its external stations. This is a geometry estimate using the existing pin locations. The alternate child needs a new bound pin map, an exact RTL miter, legal placement and timing verification. The decoder donor's own timing/LEC work remains independent.

## Rate matcher below the decoder

The retained t4 J6 placed graph contains 4,096 RM memory write flops and 512 first read-cohort flops. Every one of their existing coordinates is inside the proposed decoder or halo. I viewed [the writer relocation](s81-serdes-t4-rm-writer-fit.png) using the routed decoder's south q_d pin map and the actual 1.08 by 0.27 µm writer cell sizes. The bit-ordered soft seed needs 16 rows at y361.125–369.225 µm, between the decoder's halo and the planned coll_o0 stations at y340. Nearest horizontal cell-edge gap is 0.702 µm, and the longest pin-to-writer preview leg is 27.741 µm. Re-derive the seeds from the final front3 view and exact shell ODB before legalization. The 512 read-cohort sites still need their own active-source and egress placement.
