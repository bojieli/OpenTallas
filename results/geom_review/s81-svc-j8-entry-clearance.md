# J8 SVC entry clearance

Viewed the north t2 Rest and vertical t5 Attention repair on the approved seven-layout geometry. Teal indicates moved footprints. Rust indicates their previous west-facing edges. The dotted line marks the 10 µm entrance clearance.

The two narrow west entrances blocked 1,977 and 13,941 planned pin locations. Moving the directly affected boxes inward by 4.32 µm propagates through nearby rows to preserve their original channels. The resulting 23 Rest moves and 24 Attention moves clear all planned face pins and clock taps. Every pin window and cycle is preserved.

The guarded four-field packet is `j8_intake_guarded/j8_svc_seven_entry_clearance_fieldops.json` in the intel2 R9 workspace. It applies to the exact R9 plan and is ready for assembly review. The figure describes the plan geometry. The later bound kits require placement review.
