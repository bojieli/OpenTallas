Recommendation to the S81 die lead: take SA_2x2_RP (annealed compact 2x2 VM + locality re-partition of the 12 return trees) as the B-direction floorplan for the next die GRT + STA screen. Against GEN18c re-planned with the same method (C0) it cuts traffic x length 14% (253.6 to 217.3 M bit.um/cycle), explorer hop bit.um 8% (2.006 to 1.840 G), relay bit.stages 16% (5.22 to 4.39 M), the worst cut from 1.32 to 0.78 (windows over 0.60: 70 to 4), and the worst field round trip from 69.7 to 58.7 mm (140 to 118 stations at 500 um). One required fix before adoption: SU and VM sit on opposite sides of the 1,512 um crossing corridor, so the 17,137-bit SU-VM link is over-reach; give it explicit relay stations (about 3 stages, ~51k flops, +3 cycles on the SU path, compiler-visible) or narrow the corridor under the SU. P1_pipeline2x2_RP (architect pipeline order) is the alternate: 9 fewer field cycles, cut 0.78, but +3% traffic and two long returns (MIX4-VM 12,732 b and capture-gather 6,959 b across the corridor). The B layout as specified (C1) overlaps frames F19/F21 and cannot be built in the GEN18c hub region; RTL-MP is not competitive here. Nothing is adopted; this is a block-level screen.

One objective, every candidate

Every candidate is scored by the same code (score.py): p2p links on facing faces plus the VM internal span to its four slices; field trees as rectilinear MSTs over the frame street ports with the far half routed through the crossing corridor; traffic = width x class duty from the measured AR token path (828,878 cycles/token); one relay per 500 um; cut shares on a 200 um grid at 21.1 bits/um. Findings

  • The field trees dominate: 157-194 of 217-254 M bit.um/cycle is field class, so the corridor height and the tree roots (gather, PQ) next to it decide most of the score.
  • The compact 2x2 VM (4,168.8 x 3,715.2 um) leaves 1,755 um of the 5,946 um hub region beside it, enough for one 1,728 um column; the HC towers cannot stand beside it, so B needs the towers re-stacked (C1 overlaps frames F19/F21).
  • Re-partitioning the 12 return trees by locality is free in area (same trees, same sizes) and cuts trees spanning both halves from 11 to 3; it removes most cut hot spots (SA_2x2 12 to 4 windows over 0.60 in score.py).
  • The architect pipeline order puts SU, HC_s and capture face-to-face with the VM and gives the shortest field round trip (54.4 mm), but the column beside the VM cannot also hold gather, MIX4 and COLL; whichever is left out becomes a long return across the corridor.
  • OpenROAD rtl_macro_placer has no crossing-corridor model and pushes macros to the fence boundary; the best of six runs (boundary weight 0, wirelength weight 1000) is 27% worse in traffic x length than SA_2x2_RP with cut 1.89.
  • SA_1x4v (VM as four stacked slices, direction A) scores like SA_2x2 and keeps over-reach at the frame baseline; it is not the owner-selected direction and is kept as a reference.

Method, assumptions, unknowns

  • Screen only: block-level geometry, no pins assigned for moved hubs, no GRT. Adoption needs the die lead's die GRT + three-corner STA (TT setup, FF hold).
  • Traffic is an activity proxy (class share of the measured AR token path); no per-link S81 compiler calendar exists yet.
  • HCX projection homes, controller, gatherpack, embed expand and Engram dispatch have no die port list yet; they are placed but carry no edges.
  • Frames are fixed at GEN18c; only the hub region (x 13,592-19,538 um) was re-placed.
  • Tools: tools/s81/global_fp/{graph,score,placer,architect,rtlmp,candidates,explorer_export,register,page}.py; explorer records gfp_s81_* (import --candidate-of s81_gen18c).
