# S81 final-select production rung mapping

Candidate INDEXED13 preserves original 20-bit indices and BF16 score bits. Opt in with RTL `OT_S81PH_SEL_INDEXED13` and compiler `--bk-indexed13`. Both default OFF. The native selector arithmetic, reduction/tie policy, SRAM inventory and pipeline latency stay unchanged.

The old sparse format requires 5,983 ingress rung beats for the real L20 fixture. Packed type8 takes 324: four single HDR beats and 160 two-beat candidate words, thirteen candidates per word. Three native lanes are masked. Each tuple is score16 then index20, mask in data[480:468], last in data[481]. Keeping original indices removes any remapping RAM and preserves order and ties. A wrong-original-index witness fails even though its scores stay intact.

The no-boot production job spans 1,033 cycles to its last actual egress. Reserve **1,037** for all output slots: 324 ingress +639 intervening tail cycles +74 output slots. The reset bench drains one additional boot DONE, so its spans are 1,035 actual /1,039 reserved. The 640-cycle post-input budget is the pre-existing selector tail allowance; it is not a new pipeline latency. Legacy actual span is 6,692 (6,694 including boot).

The 178-cycle primitive price cannot include the production V-rung ingress: even the information in 2,048 score/index tuples needs at least 268 276-bit edges before protocol overhead. The old sparse-position encoding is the dominant regression; after packing, selector tail dominates. Conditional DAG cost at 1,037 cycles is AR +0.822% /MTP +0.939% versus the original price, within the per-unit accepted-cost rule. At legacy 6,692 the respective costs are +5.915% /+6.709%. These are model shares, not end-to-end RTL measurements. The default and headline numbers are not changed by this candidate record.

Transport accounting retains the existing measured TP4 topk_merge **551 cycles for 16,384 bytes** and the rank-tree **729-cycle leg**. Each candidate stays in a 64-bit upstream slot (score16 +original index20 +reserved28), so 2,048 candidates occupy 16,384 bytes /256 payload flits of 512 bits. Existing packet/header/RS and relay costs stay charged. Local repacking emits 164 logical 512-bit words /324 V beats /89,424 wire bits. No link bandwidth, header or relay cost is elided. Reserve the output drain slots and keep unrelated southbound V CTL out of them. The compiler emits the ingress/drain/output words with the opt-in flag and records `bk_indexed13` in schedule.json and the per-op plan.

**Integration gaps remain explicit:** the current die has no producer that delivers score+index candidates from TP4 to u_bk_sel.vi_s (stimulus comes from fixtures); there is no compiled runtime IDX-to-sparse-gather consumer; and downstream operation start times still use the priced schedule. The candidate cannot be adopted until the physical quarter view and those production paths qualify. The changed quarter master is `dsdv_bksel_q`, original spec `tools/ref_tile/specs_s81dvbk/s81dvbk_sq.json`. Decoder capture grows by 209 bits per quarter (836 total), and adds thirteen small score/index muxes; no memory macro is added.
