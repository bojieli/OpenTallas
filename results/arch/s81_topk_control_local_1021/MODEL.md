The SRAM primitive is existing source, not new arithmetic. With logical push at edgeE, its write register and current-hit flag can expose that write as the head immediately afterE; previous-hit forwarding covers the delayed macro write. The source must prove this for empty→push, pop/refill, full replacement, wrap and repeated same-address traffic. Empty pop is prevented by the original `take` predicate (`lv` before the edge); a simultaneous arrival does not grant a pop from an empty queue. The mechanism proof must exercise that exact predicate rather than impose an arrival restriction. The four-deep capacity and DM4 credit accounting stay unchanged.
