# Proposal: a reach-native PV16 block (option b)

KVPLAN 1815, 2026-10-16. This is a proposal for Q-KV and the PV leaf owner (Q-ATTN-RATE). The decision comes after the kvs13l die STA.

## Why the trunked successor (option a) is expensive

The trunked pair `qkd_pvw_die_t` is in `plan.json` in this directory and is generated by `tools/qwen_kv_die/pv_trunk_gen.py`. It keeps today's PV block: 3 x 3 closed tiles, 8 slices around the join, 561.6 um square, with 21.6-um internal gaps.

That block was laid out for unregistered die wires, and three of its properties make registered distribution expensive:

1. **Inputs face both block edges.** Every slice takes all of its inputs (pv2 / pe2 / bc) on its S face:
   - the bottom-row tiles face the channel below the block;
   - the top-row tiles face the channel above it;
   - the middle-row tiles (banks 2 / 7) face the 21.6-um internal gaps, so they are reached through a neighbour tile.

   As a result every block row needs a station channel on both sides: 17 channels for 16 rows.
2. **Middle-row reset pins sit deep in the block.** The middle-row reset pins sit on the W face at mid height, 240-320 um inside the block. They need their own 1-b stations.
3. **The join's sf[7:4] pins are deep too.** They sit 361 um above the block bottom, so the bank-6 fault cannot reach them within 400 um. This is the only residual of option (a): 32 one-bit wires at 455-475 um.

With >= 20-um macro clearance and <= 400-um hops, this forces:
- 172.8-um channels (two 296 rows plus a 36 row);
- a 345.6-um CT, holding a per-column copy of the V vertical chains so that the far tile column stays within two channel hops.

## Proposal

Re-lay each column so that every slice input face and every join control pin lies on one channel. The PV leaf RTL is unchanged; only the column plan and the join's pins change.

- **Column = 2 rows of 4 slices around one shared channel.**
  - The lower row is MX, so its S faces point up.
  - The upper row is R0, so its S faces point down.
  - Both rows' input faces then abut the same channel.
  - The join sits at the end of the channel.
  - The ts faces of the join (ts0..ts3) are re-pinned so that each pair of slices reaches it across the channel. That needs a join re-close; it is already needed for sf[7:4].
- **One channel per column**, instead of one channel per block edge.
  - Every slice is <= ~90 um from its station, and every station is fed along the channel.
  - The per-tile reset and sf stations collapse into the face stations, because the reset pin moves to the S face in a slice re-pin.
- **Column footprint:** about 4 x 194.4 = 777.6 um wide, 2 x 172.8 + one ~90-um channel = ~436 um tall, plus the join end cell. The 32 columns tile as 2 x 16 with the CT trunk between the two column stacks. The V and control trunks then need only the 16 channel crossings, not 17 channels with 2 faces each.

## Estimated savings against option (a)

These are first-order estimates; they are to be confirmed by running the generator on the new block plan.

- **Height.** 16 column rows x ~436 um = ~7.0 mm, plus 16 x ~22 um clearances, plus the strip: ~7.5 mm, against 12.0 mm in option (a) (9.5 mm today).
- **Width.** 2 x ~800 um (columns including the join cell) + CT ~220 um = ~1.8 mm, against 1.47 mm in option (a) (1.14 mm today).
- **Area.** The frame is ~13.5 mm2 against 17.6 mm2 for option (a), about 4 mm2 less per pair and ~8 mm2 for both. The die height grows ~2 mm less than in option (a); the column gets ~0.35 mm wider. Net die area is roughly **+35-40 mm2**, against +68 mm2 for option (a).
- **Flops.** About half of option (a): no double-face channels, no per-tile reset stations, one A_v per column.
- **Latency.** Shallower: about 2 stages per row pitch against 3, so ~35 edges against 51 on column 0.

## Cost and risk

- Re-closing three tiles:
  - slice re-pin: rst_n to the S face (the current RESET_PIN flop stays);
  - join re-pin: ts faces plus sf;
  - control tile: TRUNK_D / FLAG_D at the new depths.
- A new column plan from the PV owner, then a regenerated pair.
- Same bench: per-column bit-exact output at its static age, with mutant FAIL.

## Recommendation

1. Run kvs13l on option (a) now; it is the only one that is ready.
2. Ask the PV owner to cost option (b) using the kvs13l die STA and route data.
3. Adopt (b) if its re-closes finish within the closure window.
