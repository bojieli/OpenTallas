## Internal reservations and external abstract semantics

The [LEF/DEF5.8 language reference, Macro Obstruction and Pin Port statements](https://coriolis.lip6.fr/doc/lefdef/lefdefref/LEFSyntax.html) defines OBS as routing blockages and PORT shapes as access geometries. Separate PORTs of one PIN represent internal resistive connectivity; power routers are expected to connect every PORT. A defined port can override an overlapping obstruction under the language's contained-via conditions. EXCEPTPGNET is an explicit separate syntax. No such exception or additional internal PIN is introduced here.

Consequently the121 internal M1 electrical interfaces and their M1–M3 exclusion cells are internal core design boundaries, not121 exposed PG MTerms or parent access sites. The prototype's363 lower OBS rectangles and twelve broad OBS are retained reservations; their overlap with source-owned internal conductor does not turn them into actual metal or create an electrical short. The121 old M4 PG/OBS attachment conflicts remain explicitly HOLD for native abstract/export interpretation. They are not waived by net equality or by the independent internal connectivity graph. Only the real N/S M5 subsets are proposed external PG access: two PORTs per VDD/VSS per master. They do not overlap retained M5 OBS. A native diagnostic must separately establish actual external access, blockage interpretation, allowed widths/tracks and via-in-port behavior. Textual LEF semantics and this model establish no native AP PASS.
