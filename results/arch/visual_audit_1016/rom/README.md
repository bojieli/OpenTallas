# VIS-ROM visual layout audit, 2026-10-16

Owner order (2026-10-16 ~01:00 PT): crowding and pin-positioning problems must be fixed. Inspect the geometry at both tile and die level, the way a physical designer would.

Scope: the three ROM dies. These are the Qwen compute die, the Qwen KV/system die and the DeepSeek S81 die. Every master that was not "closed + native PASS" in `results/arch/die_completeness_1015/completeness.json` is in scope, plus newer successor jobs. The latest die-level runs and plans are covered too: Qwen compute r2b2 (GRT running) and the r2f2 plan, Qwen KV kvs10/kvs11 (case/PDN), and S81 c21h (GRT running) with the VM-centred v2 plan.

Every image listed was LOOKED at. This audit reports only; the owning streams do the fixes.

- `findings.json`: 55 findings (severity 1/2/3 = {'1': 12, '2': 30, '3': 13}). Each finding gives the master, job, image, what was seen, the numbers, the class and the structural fix. Masters with no layout to inspect are listed under `no_image`.
- `sheets/contact_*_tiles.png`: the worst ~12 tiles on each die, labelled. `sheets/contact_die_*.png`: one sheet per die.
- `img/`: the 1-3 most telling images for each finding.
- `tools/`: the scripts used to take each snapshot. They are read-only on the remote runs. `tile_snap.sh` dumps the most advanced odb in a run with `tools/ref_tile/layout_dump.tcl` and renders it with `layout_png.py`; it also collects the ORFS `final_*.webp` images. `tile_stats.py` measures pins per face, layers, pins/um, slivers and density. `die_dump.sh`, `die_stats.py`, `die_links.py` and `region_empty.py` cover the die level (blocks, links and empty regions).

Classes: P1 pin face points away from its consumer; P2 pins crowded on one edge or layer; P3 narrow channel or no room for glue logic; P4 hot congestion window; P5 empty area, or utilisation above 60%; P6 clock/reset entry or a long chain.

## Top findings
1. **S81 relay faces come from block centres, not pin positions** (`tools/dsrom_s81_fulldie.py` fc() at ~L6986, and the forced opposite face). On rly_283_EW/266_EW (3,042 instances), every input bundle wraps around the tile. This is the native launch failure of 243/237 ps. The same root cause makes several small relays face away 100% of the time. Owner: S-DIE.
2. **Qwen NK tile W glue squeeze is still not fixed:** a 20.3 um band carries 2,076 W pins, and 145 of the 170 DPL cells are in it. The r3 change touches fences only. Owner: Q-COMPUTE-DIE.
3. **Whitespace at die level.** The S81 hub band is 161 of 315 mm2 white. The Qwen compute die has 95.7 mm2 empty (centre column plus the south strips). The Qwen KV spine is empty, and the split-PV plan uses it, but the south spine is 1,287 um wide against the 1,598 um needed.
4. **Single-net resets:** Qwen n_rst_field has 1,585 terminals (r2b2 was a relay-skip diagnostic; full-relay runs r2h1/r2h2 include the reset tree). S81 n_rs_col has 451 terminals per column. Abstract pins are INOUT on xcol_w and qbank_N.
5. **Pin walls in many tiles** (xs_tap 20.9 pins/um on 26.5 um, qkd_rly_* 20.9 pins/um, bf_front, ectl_a, ucie_gearbox), combined with clock entries in corners. Also: dsfd_host_c has 4 unbalanced clock trees (23.6k hold violations), and dsfd_cfifo is bound without its xd2/xf2 ports (68,864 floating bits).
