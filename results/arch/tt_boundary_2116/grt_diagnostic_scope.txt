Scale tg1 attempt 1 failed GRT-0183 on _04208_. The actual CTS ODB inspected during the preserved retry has this net crossing bank 1's M1-M4 footprint: driver scale_addr[431] QN at x146.448..147.528,y161.73..162.0 and sink inverter A at x25.974..26.136,y161.19..161.46; SRAM bank 1 x32.4..127.224,y129.6..170.64. Endpoint neighborhoods are sparse, with no local CTS obstructions or placement blockages. Pre-GRT macro-edge guards and routing capacities are not yet measured in this artifact, so the SRAM crossing is geometry evidence, not a proven root cause.
Potential structural candidate: move both SRAMs to the east free face (scale has 602 west-facing signal pins, no east-facing signal pins), keeping the same two real 94.824x41.04 macros, outline, RTL and cycles. The SRAM LEF puts clk, addresses, rd_out and write data on its west face; R0 is the facing orientation for an east memory column.
Do not adopt from the error message alone. The pinned router's GRT-0183 fires when the 3D search heap empties, and the upstream maintainer notes that past occurrences also came from router bugs.
