# T3 coverage: DeepSeek-V4.1 on the HBM accelerator (r25 die, TP-96, AR + DSpark MTP)

Stream cov-T3, 2026-10-08 PT. This pass follows `claude-takeover-20261007/coverage/METHOD.md`. It only finds gaps; nothing was fixed or launched.

The ledger is `T3.json`: 96 nodes, 61 data and 35 control.

## How the graph was derived

The graph was built top-down from the model, not from the hardware lists.

**Model sources**
- `compiler/models/deepseek-v4.1-flash/inference_config.json`. Key facts:
  - 40 layers;
  - compress ratios 0 / 2 / 1;
  - KV sources 2 / 8 / 14 / 20;
  - index layers 2 / 8 / 14 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32 / 36;
  - candidate source 20;
  - Engram on layers 1 and 14;
  - hc_mult 4 with 20 Sinkhorn iterations;
  - 384 experts, top-6, plus 1 shared;
  - DSpark: 3 stages, block 5, targets 37–39, drafter top-3 of 128.
- The golden `tools/hdc_golden_v41.py`: `Model.layer`, `attention`, `indexer`, `compressor`, `moe`, `forward_positions`, `draft`, `generate_spec` and `truncate`.
- The TP-96 ISA `tools/w19_hbm_tp96_isa.py`: `compile_layer` and `compile_head`. Its records are `results/rtl/w19_hbm_tp96_isa*.json`, which pass at ISA level only.

**Hardware sources**
- `tools/hbm_accel_die_fp.py` (ADOPTED = R25).
- `physical/hbm_accel_die_views/index.json` (r25 views).
- `/api/elements`, read at 21:37 PT (271 rows).
- The MTP branches and the mtp-hbm, mtp-exact and safe-hbm logs.

**Cycle sources**
- `results/arch/token_path_20261008/hbm_ds.json` (AR, 699,175.7 cycles, 1,716.3 tok/s).
- `results/arch/token_path_20261008/hbm_ds_mtp.json` (step 1,348,759.6 cycles, 3,700.3 tok/s at τ 4.159).

**Mapping check.** Every one of the 2,291 AR token-path nodes maps to a ledger node (checked by label). Summing the 80 parallel `hcp` nodes reproduces the total. Ledger nodes with `in_token_path = null` are model operators or control functions that the token path does not draw.

**No token-path node has every one of its die elements closed.** Two elements carry most of the cycles and are both open:
- `hfd_sm` is interim: post-CTS with no routed whole-SM database, `smh tile_e` / `tile_w` / `front_c` failing.

Of the AR cycles, 36,653 (5.2 %) sit on operators whose hardware is on no die master: the indexer and the expert steering. A further 357,348 (51 %, overlapping the open blocks above) rest partly on vendor or modelled switch terms.

## Gaps ranked by token-path impact

1. **The indexer is not on the die (NOT_ON_DIE).** Nodes T3-D28 and D30–D34. Cost: 29,308 AR cycles (4.2 %) over 8 layers, plus 6× that per MTP verify.
   - The six `hfd_index_q_b*` bands are an INTERIM SHELL, "NOT the indexer" (`physical/hbm_accel_die_views/index_q/rtl/hfd_index_q.sv`). They hold pipes plus a placeholder `t_vm = key[511:0]^key[1023:512]`, with no scoring, top-k or selector.
   - The real `ot_hbm_accel_index_path` scorer is ~5.9 mm² a quarter, against a 5.14 mm² slot.
   - The four "closed" bands therefore close nothing functional. Placing the real indexer grows the hub band, and the die is already height-limited (1.38 mm margin; COMPLETENESS_AUDIT §3).
   - Owner: HBM die-views / index agent, with mtp-die for the floorplan.
2. **MTP KV rollback is not exact against the as-built HBM write path (MISSING_HW + NOT_EXACT + NOT_ON_DIE).** Nodes T3-P12 and P09. This affects the 3,700.3 MTP headline's correctness.
   - `ot_dshbm_spec_state` makes rollback a single write of `n`. That is exact only if every window writer and reader uses its WR = 136 ring (W + PMAX).
   - The as-built `ot_hbm_accel_dskv_wb` and its read stream use ring slot `pos mod 128`.
   - So a rejected row at r overwrites the row of r − 128, which the next committed position (r − 5 ≤ p < r) still reads.
   - No bench connects spec_state to the HBM write-back or read path. spec_state is itself blocked (spec3_s8 has 2 mismatches; spec_state_f is in flight on mtp-exact).
   - The 6-position verify write-back is also unpriced.
   - Owner: mtp-exact (bench) plus mtp-hbm / svc owner (dskv_wb ring parameter and read-stream addressing).
3. **There is no KV / CKV / index-key write path on the die (NOT_ON_DIE).** Nodes T3-D20 and D25. This affects AR correctness on every token.
   - `dskv_wb` is exact (768/768) but is in no master.
   - The r25 stream service accepts only read commands (kind 3 is "reserved, dropped").
   - It is priced at 0 (posted). The worst stream interference of 2.03 µs a stack and the serial bound of 2.37 µs a token are not charged.
   - Owner: HBM svc / die-views.
4. **The hc_mixes projection (HCP) and Sinkhorn are not on the die (NOT_ON_DIE).** Nodes T3-D09 and D10. There are 80 instances a token at 0.793 µs each (76,128 parallel cycles), drawn as exposed 0.
   - `hfd_hc` holds only the 88 hc_post lanes. The generator charges hc as a 6.09 mm² model area.
   - The Sinkhorn figure was measured at 151.9 MHz on the ROM hub.
   - If the HCP cannot run beside the body on its own unit, up to 63 µs a token is exposed.
   - Owner: HBM hub quarter (hc).
5. **Off-path model judgements that are absent from the token path (NOT_PRICED).** Nodes T3-D02, D03, D05, D07, D23, D24 and D31.
   - The missing work:
     - Engram hashing and the token-history holder (no die block);
     - the `engram.rows_gather` and `engram.kv_gather` collectives (4 a token);
     - compressor pooling + `indexer.wk` + k_norm + RoPE + FP4 on the owner die (4 KV-source layers);
     - the compressor open-group state;
     - the layer-20 candidate merge collective + cand_apply.
   - All are 0 µs in `dshbm_1m_allmeasured` ("model judgement").
   - Estimate if exposed: about 5 collectives × ~1,060 cycles, plus the owner-die chains.
   - Owner: HBM composition (`tools/dshbm_1m_allmeasured.py`, `tools/dshbm_matched_reference.py`).
6. **No producer of the RoPE cos/sin tables (MISSING_HW).** Node T3-D19.
   - The norm/RoPE chains (`ot_dsrom_su_norm`, `ot_ds_hbm_cluster20_integrated norm_cos_t`) take the tables as top-level inputs, and benches tie them off.
   - Four tables are needed a token: plain at pos, YaRN at pos, YaRN at pos + 1 − r, and the indexer q.
   - Owner: HBM SU agent.
7. **Host loop and stop condition (NOT_PRICED / MISSING_HW).** Nodes T3-H01, H02, C02 and S05.
   - In AR the position comes from a host doorbell every token, through the cmdproc. The completion → host → doorbell to all 96 dies is not a token-path node, and there is no fan-out design.
   - No die or MTP block compares EOS. The dspark ctl caps only `cfg_ngen` and MAXPOS.
   - Owner: HBM cmdproc / host-link.
8. **The GPU-prefill KV ingest has no hardware (MISSING_HW + NOT_PRICED).** Node T3-H03. This is an owner requirement.
   - The only path is the generic 64-bit loader DMA (`hfd_loader`, failing at TT −363.8; host SS −3,672 ps).
   - Nothing formats the dskv_wb layout: the ring slots, the CKV / IK owner die `(n/8) mod 96`, the open key-block shadow, the compressor slots, the token history or the DSK rows.
   - The ~0.9 GB of a 1M user's ingest is unpriced.
   - Owner: HBM loader.
9. **MTP control is still off the die and partly modelled.** Nodes T3-P04–P16.
   - `hfd_mtp` / `hfd_mtp_x` are on their first route (`claude/mtp-hbm-20261008` d71a24143). The SS-era blocks failed TT re-STA: accept −125.5, ctl −364.0, spec_a0 −377.5 internal, fence −613.6.
   - The expert union is MODELLED_ONLY: 91,428 cycles, 6.8 % of the step, from W19 and a lower bound.
   - Draft transport is modelled (21,853 cycles), and full FEC is not charged on the 23 draft crossings.
   - The seed's capture of the layer-37–39 inputs is not drawn.
   - The DSK ring write has no HBM write path.
   - Owners: mtp-hbm (blocks), mtp-die (placement in the recipe), composition (union walk, draft FEC).
10. **Every token-path block is unclosed (NOT_CLOSED).**
    - SM (17 % of AR cycles): tile_e, tile_w and front_c are failing; the hfd_sm view is interim.
    - Collective endpoint (41 %).
    - SU, SFU and norm groups (12 %).
    - Attention tile (4 %): SS −2,032 ps. R25A does not fit; R25S is flag-off.
    - VM8 sub-tiles (6.8 %).
    - `hfd_hc`: TT −3,474.
    - `hfd_router`: revoked, FF −69.
    - `hfd_cmdproc_n/s`: revoked.
    - svc SE_s6 / SW_s4: revoked. 13 of 17 svc segments have no r25 view.
    - The unified-ledger gates are still gated-unknown: sm_su_result_edge_native, vm_alignment, su_reducer_safe, su_c12_margin, su_full_ddiv31, cdc / credit, die_clock_plan and hbm_full_die.
11. **Expert dispatch (NOT_ON_DIE).** Node T3-D45.
    - Gate row "corrected+wg" credits expert-workgroup steering, but the generator lists its descriptor logic as "lever L1, not built".
    - Routed-expert fetch reaches 86.0 % of peak, against the owner's ≥ 90 % rule.
12. **Boot and fault infrastructure.**
    - B04: no PLL macro (MISSING_HW).
    - B05: no reset / bring-up sequencer (NOT_ON_DIE).
    - B01 / B02 / B03: weight, Engram-table and program load are unpriced and have no bench.
    - C06: no link retry or replay (MISSING_HW); FEC is vendor.
    - F01: HBM ECC is the vendor's.
    - F02: on-die SRAM protection is not selected.
    - F03: protected ingress is not in hfd_coll.
    - F04: no fault / trap bench.
13. **Vendor and modelled terms.** Node T3-C05.
    - The Tomahawk Ultra tier: 28.7 % of AR cycles on vendor TU budgets plus 6.8 % on the modelled striping tail.
    - The HBM PHY + controller is a vendor abstract with assumed boundary timing.
    - Both are labelled as such and are not hardware gaps of ours. C05 overlaps the per-collective tail cycles that are already listed under D17 / D38 / D39 / D43 / D48 / D51 / S04.

## Checked, not gaps

- SM column count: NC 8 ≥ 6 verify positions (mtp-hbm 21:23).
- The static weight / KV stream reaches 96.2 % of peak.
- `dskv_wb` addressing is exact against DRAM timing, including the read-back.
- The control-plane RTL (dspark_ctl / accept / argmax / union) is bit-exact against `generate_spec` at component level.
- The ISA-level TP-96 token (AR and MTP, gather and oreduce) passes against the released-checkpoint golden.
- The Engram tables fit in HBM.
