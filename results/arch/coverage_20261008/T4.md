**Answers to the specific questions:**
- **GQA:** the r25 attention tile (`hfd_attn_tile` = H16 `ot_attn_tile_m6h1q` quads) broadcasts one KV row to all 16 heads. That is DS MQA: 64 heads on one latent.
  - At TP4 a die holds 2 KV heads × 4 q heads, so a tile can serve one KV head with 4 of its 16 head lanes (25%) unless the mapping changes.
  - The datapath is functionally general, but no GQA addressing or bench exists.
- **QK-norm:** there is no fused support. DS SU fused KIND 1 normalises the DS latent; per-head 128-wide norms with a gain would have to be an SU program, which nobody has written or benched.
- **RoPE:** **unsupported as built.**
  - DS rotates the last 64 elements as adjacent pairs (YaRN, `ROPE_PAIR 32`).
  - Qwen needs rotate_half over all 128 dims (pairs i, i+64; theta 1e6).
- **Head dim 128:** supported by slicing (two 64-element TD slices; DS uses 8).
- **Dense FFN:** it is **not** on the MoE engine at top-k = 1. On r25 a dense FFN is a plain SM matvec on the layer weight stream, like the DS shared expert, and the router and expert fetch are bypassed.
  - **But the SM has no INT8 weight decode.** It supports E4M3 / E2M1 block-dot against E4M3 block-quantised activations, or BF16 × BF16 lanes. Qwen's verified contract is INT8 per-row × BF16.
- **Vocab 151,936:** **unsupported.** `ot_dshbm_argmax` has `IW = 17` (131,072 entries) and the DS DSpark control has `TW = 17`. Both need 18 bits.
- **RMSNorm:** the hardened norm engine is fixed at D = 5,120 with the hyper-connection mix and an FP8 output. Qwen needs D = 4,096 and BF16. eps is a port and 2^12 is in the divide set.
- **Softmax / SwiGLU:**
  - The DS softmax carries an attention-sink term. Disabling it is not a verified mode.
  - The fused SwiGLU clamps and multiplies by a route weight (both can be neutralised through ports or parameters). It also FP8-quantises its output, which Qwen does not.
- **Arithmetic order:** the r25 attention tile and the SU follow the DS golden's chunk8 / pairwise order. Bit-exactness against the Qwen golden therefore needs a Qwen golden for r25's arithmetic, or r25 modes that reproduce the W12 order.

## 4. What a Qwen-on-HBM token-path export needs

1. An owner decision on which machine T4 is.
   - Under the unified r25 die, gaps 2-10 must be closed first.
   - If the owner instead accepts the Qwen HBM tile die, it needs closure elements and die evidence.
2. A Qwen decode program for the chosen die. On r25 that means the cmdproc20 ISA, with a weight-stream segment table and SRAM residency.
3. One exact RTL stage per layer type plus the head at P8191 (owner rule), against a golden in the die's arithmetic. Vehicle A's `qwen_hbmacc_p8191_20261004` is the template: compose as 7 + Σ stages + 36.
4. A `qwen_hbm()` function in `tools/token_path_export.py` with classes for the HBM stream, SM, SU, attention, collective and head. Each class lists its r25 elements and a die-wire re-price, as `hbm()` does for DS (`results/rtl/hbm_accel_die_floorplan_20261005/wire_stages*.json` already carries a `qwen_8k` transfer).
5. A matching `qwen_hbm` target in `tools/unified_composition.py` and `tools/reprice_20261008.py`, so the export's totals can be asserted against a published rate.
6. MTP: a `qwen_hbm_mtp` view only after an owner decision and r25 drafter/verify RTL. The current 5,055 tok/s is vehicle A only and uses an off-rule τ.
