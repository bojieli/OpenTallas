## Source-rule calendar model: corrected holds matter

The tiny readiness model implements the actual idle walk, start hold, staged record hold, record invalidation, registered SRAM/forwarded Q, current fresh qualification, registered compare and fire. It enumerates all pointer phases for every active size2..47. This is an abstract readiness calculation, **not execution of full HWIN RTL**. Its selected slot becomes coherent on the actual previous-write-forwarding edge; raw OLD SRAM data is never assumed equal to a same-edge write.

For47 slots, both source holds reduce worst first-head readiness to47 edges after the staged record; one capture stage raises it to48. Accepted-start timestamp is one edge before staged rec_slot visibility, so the candidate readiness corresponds toD49, the original47-slot minimum. A discarded recurrence omitted `hold=(st&&pe&ss)||(pe&rec_slot)` and reported49→50 after record. That recurrence contradicts source and is recorded as a **rejected model mutant**, never as evidence that early capture is impossible.

The real boundary requiring work is **two slots**: baseline first-head readiness3 after record mapsD4; a naive additional capture needs4 after record, mapsD5. Original minimumD4 is legal. This bounded source-rule witness means simple capture/lookahead cannot inherit all original configurations without an additional mechanism. It is not yet a source-level RTL failure log. A potential zero-date alternative captures coherent registered `wdata` one edge before normal forwarded Q, when `wv`, write/head address, record generation and validity prove identity. This would require useful bypass mux/predicates and complete source-control proof; raw SRAM prefetch is wrong under an OLD collision. No bypass is implemented or costed as zero gates.

Early capture must advance the captured comparison-time horizon by one: capture head/time at k, compare at k+1 using captured_time+1, then keep the original output edge at k+2. It must carry both attributes, pointer/generation, okay/fresh and qualified forwarding, and revalidate current pointer, record invalidation, vld/fd and reset at use. No speculative next read may evict a live current head from the sole1R port. The current source already captures output payload/winning selects alongside its lookahead; these must move as a whole. The existing16 depth1/2 head bundles are independent state, not a new indexed >2kb store; any depth>=3 queue must be a real ring/SRAM.

First-head readiness alone does **not** prove late-fault dates, retirement, boot acceptance, replay priority or occupied restart. Keep original D/gap/return-limit fixtures and exact dated causal mutants. In particular, at a newly fresh already-late head, a historical head compare can discover late one edge later unless its bypass and late predicate are treated explicitly. Same-edge event/record/retire collisions must use the same original generation, and not a payload from a subsequently overwritten slot.

## Full added-edge alternative is a composed contract

A full read capture can always be *proposed* as+1 local compare edge; there is no certificate here that the complete public ABI is+1. `calendar_obligations.json` names every coupled state and observable action. Delaying just `eqn/ltn` changes fire, pointer advance, fd, output winners and collision fault. Delaying just retire changes vld/counts, RX outstanding membership and boot acceptance. Neither option is a throughput-free date rewrite. Full output/start/retire/RX/boot/PINPIPE/C/token deltas remainNULL until the chosen dependency graph is evaluated.
