Recommendation to H-DIE

Adopt the mid-block VM band with NPG4 engines (T_band2__npg4_s5) as the v15 no-ATT floorplan direction for die GRT + TT/FF STA. Per quadrant: PHY and the 8 PC groups on the die edge, a 2×4 SM block (R0/MY), and a 1.8 mm VM band between SM rows 2 and 3 that holds the 8-bank slice, LG chains, SU/SFU/HC/QUANT port groups, the hub and COLL. Every SM is at most two SM rows from its slice.

vs v15/D minus ATT
Σ bits·mm −32%, DS traffic·length −55%, Qwen −3%, needed relay flops −34%, bits over 500 µm −15%, LG fixed-return violations 24 → 0, worst cut 1.05 → 0.556.
Structural cost
SW/SE SU/SFU/HC at 4 bank-pair port groups (BANK_GROUP 0x33221100) instead of the provisional 5 (contract v15.1 NPG5). No area change; compiler bank-group table change. Without it the same band scores cut 0.636.
KV home
Stack-local: dense KV sequence-sliced to the own-stack SM, DS top-k rows rank-assigned to SMs and compressed KV replicated in all 4 stacks (+3 row writes per layer per token, +3× KV capacity). Removes cross-quadrant KV traffic; the interleaved single copy scores within noise on wires but carries 75% of top-k bytes over the VM ring.
Long returns kept
VM ring and COLL ring seams 12.5–15 mm (2×2 ring between quadrant hubs: algorithmic, relayed); HBM→hub delivery up to 15 mm for the far PC groups; single NE HC engine 16.7 mm (per-quadrant HC engine costs +3 mm² and did not win here).
Fix before pins
Hub faces exceed pin capacity (vmh_SW/NW 1.19, SE/NE 1.07 at 10 pins/µm): grow the hub outline to ≥1.1 mm or adopt the 8-lane delivery split already being hardened.
Owner option
The freed ATT area allows a 660 mm² outline (20.0 mm tall, −197 mm²) that also meets cut 0.574 and fr 0. That is a whole-design change; reported, not adopted.
Not shown
RTL-MP and the free annealer both lose: RTL-MP pulls SMs off their stacks; the free annealer shortens wires but breaks the cut and two LG fixed returns.

Published with this page: per-block pin positions (one segment per link end on the face nearest its peer, 1-D packed) and the operator → block map (ATT.QK/PV now map to SM + PC + VM + SU). Both are registered as data-flow explorer candidates of hbm_v15_compact.

What the graph assumes

  • Nodes: 32 SM frames, 32 PC groups, 4 PHYs, 32 VM banks with LG chains, 4 hubs, SU/SFU/HC port groups, QUANT on NW, 4 COLL engines, 9 SerDes + endpoints, IDX parts, HC engine. 444 mm² of blocks.
  • Links: 715, widths from the v15.1 contract and SM frame ports; traffic from the compiled Qwen3-8B TP4 8K and DS-V4.1-Flash TP96 1M programs. Attention adds KV rows on the SM HBM port, q/P on x-load, scores and PV partials on SM out, softmax on SU, and a new 64-bit KV descriptor port per SM.
  • Lengths are closest-outline gaps (pins unassigned), the same optimistic rule for every candidate. Block-level evidence only: die GRT and three-corner STA remain the qualification.
