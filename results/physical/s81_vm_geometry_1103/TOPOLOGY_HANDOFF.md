Three smallest useful source-derived placement units are now explicit:

- Each capture lane plus its two related CDC halves is a 345.6µm square R0 atom: cap [0,86.4], writer [194.4,0], reader [259.2,0]. Together with seven other CDC pairs, all 48,857 internal bit legs are ≤424.128µm. No ports, pin order, logic, edge or orientation change. Related-clock STA and all external incident legs remain open.
- Read data should be owned by physical slice, not grouped solely by logical read destination. Each physical slice supplies exactly sixteen read data leaves; place them with its four return lanes and four FSR leaves. The eight valid leaves aggregate across slices. A return lane itself consumes strided banks from all four q8 bridges, so moving that return alone cannot make every incoming bank leg local.
- The reviewed q8 pin choices are W-output capture for banks0..3, E-output for banks4..7, and source-bound south-drain E/W storage plans. Those are explicit physical candidates, not an adopted master remap. The actual write service is 53 source records → one header / one control / four 128-bit payload roots → sixteen dispatches → 128 storage leaves. Every payload output bit fans to four dispatches; header bits fan to sixteen, and control lane bits can fan to 32 sink pins. The 53-slot bit map is now exact, including the capture slot discontinuity 32..43 and 48..51, and collective slots 44..47.

Closing the cap atom does not close capture distribution. Each complete 512-bit cap stream output feeds all four W0 payload regions in 128-bit portions. A compact frontend at one location and a tall four-slice memory arrangement therefore retain long whole-packet legs. Physical quartering of a frontend would require actual RTL, clock/reset, protocol and equivalence work; a staged distribution alternative requires real hard relays and uniform row/mask/data/valid ages. Neither is invented in these plans.

`boundary_rank_producer_local.json` records all measured producer/consumer master ports and clock domains, actual source-bit fanout, coordinate crossing-net widths, and uniform packet age lower bounds at 500µm per leg. The producer-local vertical candidate still has 177,504 of 451,846 internal functional legs above 500µm. Its cap mask → W0 leg reaches 12833.178µm; a 500µm hop rule alone implies at least 25 extra registered stages for that packet class. This candidate is rejected as a completed geometry contract. The full timing/root/hold/calendar/token ledger can only worsen or refine those geometric bounds.

Area packing is a separate result: all 640 known rectangles fit 4514.4×4903.2 after filling holes and adding a 540µm strip, but its 213,441 over-500µm legs are worse than the initial larger arrangement. The producer-local vertical candidate 3542.4×9828.0 reduces total pin L1 and failing bit count while worsening maximum distance. Both are tradeoffs, not adopted designs.
