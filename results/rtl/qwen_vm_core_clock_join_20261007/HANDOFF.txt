Minimum source-derived core clock and acceptance join

The input snapshot is elements' current composed raw-write/direct-lease core, SHA25608a60f68209b7ca00c4d5df684a353b742f1e98ea54afc355d2caa52502ba183. The extraction copies literal PINREG state, original intent and effective enable equations, actual ot_hdc_cg generate, fb_me_go/me_go, MEIF pending/take/status registers and status masking. Surrounding instruction and arithmetic datapaths are omitted. Legal directed supply/issue stimuli are exposed; the accepted counter is test instrumentation, not claimed engine RTL.

The source slice uses the real native parent ICG and actual protected provider with DIRECT_READBACK1. NR1/NW1 is deliberately the minimum request aperture needed for this clock mechanism; all256 data and32 check SRAM macros remain present. This does not replace the separate full2256/865 port/data proof or transfer its performance costs.

PINREG0 positive: queue actual MEIF go, capture a non-ME write frame with original intent0, then raise original supply while physical service runs. The real checkedACK permits one native edge with lease0; the actual ME ICG stays closed and pending go/take bookkeeping is retained. The next paid ME edge takes once. A second captured ME frame remains held after actual checkedACK until original supply returns.

PINREG1 positive: original readiness remains pr_mmo_q while native clock is held. Native-only admission with captured intent0 samples new raw readiness but cannot execute an unpaid ME edge. The subsequent leased edge accepts once. A captured ME frame uses its old sampled supply until the next native edge, preserving the existing PINREG latency.

Disabling VM_OWNED_LEASE is an actual control mutation: it produces UNPAID_SOURCE_ME_CLOCK on the native-only admission and is rejected. Both positives use two real postverified ACKs and check that payloads are known. Original smaller-prefix logs remain as history.
