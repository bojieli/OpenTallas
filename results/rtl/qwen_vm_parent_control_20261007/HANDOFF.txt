Native parent control and replay calendar — bounded finding, not bank adoption

The current core emitter was run on current ot_hdc_core_vector_weight.sv. The
native sequencer region from --sequencer through before --units is extracted.
Its real fetch FIFO, NEXT register decode, dynamic offsets, dispatch, issue,
END/drained equation and clocked state processes execute in Icarus. Child
ready/idle/progress inputs are exposed; memory service gates are held good and
unused numeric argmax values tied zero. The model is not a complete core.

The exact immutable L20 instruction words PC19 (SU), PC20 (ME) and PC25 (END)
form the deliberately reduced test program; original PCs21–24 and all earlier
work are omitted. SU replay is recaptured from the prior native SU control
component with progress and progress_rows now observed. Its arithmetic remains
unobserved as in that component's explicit scope. ME write addresses/masks and
latencies are replayed from the physical native ME address slice. Its accepting
edge is native cycle5; first/last writes 496/503 become relative ages491/498.

Observed positive replay calendar:
- SU accepts on parent edge10; its 64 write beats occupy edges31–94.
- ME accepts on edge95 at su_progress64 while su_idle is still0.
- ME write beats occupy edges586–593 (48 native masked word ports per beat).
- 640 masked word writes are recorded, preserving actual addresses/masks.
- This pair permits ME issue before SU drain; its write families do not overlap.
  This does not imply other pairs have no read/write or write/write overlap.

ME idle was NOT captured by the available ME slice. Two explicit external drain
replay ages,501 and700, test the native END wait path and produce completion
edges597 and796. Neither is a measured native drain latency or a performance
claim. These externally selected input cases do not qualify the ME idle logic.
A separate forged-early-ME-idle negative triggers END while future captured ME
writes remain. The bench rejects this based on known outstanding writes rather
than the selected drain age. Native END correctly respects the supplied idle;
correct child idle remains an unproven assumption. Neither MX nor reducer writer
is active in this selected replay. Their previous source-bound busy inclusion
must still be validated, not inferred from this replay.
