# Default-OFF declared partition tolerance consumer proposal

Exact native stops are preserved. Frozen703 eshim_tx and n3_turn partition budget.json and clock_binding.json declare TT450/FF342 with insertion_tol_ps40. Their postcts_gate.tcl consumes only OT_POSTCTS_TOL or its hard-coded20 default. The external runner exports no binding tolerance. This is a source tolerance-plumbing discrepancy, separate from whether an insertion band is an authorized gate or a design target. The helper and consumer do **not** decide that authority question.

`partition_clock_binding.py` resolves only an ordinary partition with a caller-pinned freeze SHA and expected type. It requires partition freeze/budget schemas; same die/type/outline/module across freeze, budget, binding and physical design config; exact physical pin inventory hash; agreement of both TT and FF bounds and declared tolerance; finite positive numeric values; and the observed consumer bound matching its declared corner. Core.json/master_slice contexts and SS are refused. An explicit environment tolerance may tighten the declared tolerance but cannot loosen it. Missing/ambiguous/malformed context fails closed.

The postCTS consumer is disabled unless **OT_POSTCTS_BINDING_TOL=1**, and additionally needs OT_POSTCTS_PARTITION_TYPE plus OT_POSTCTS_PARTITION_FREEZE_SHA256. With the flag absent or0, legacy behavior stays exactly as before. Future generated kits contain the helper; no existing generated/frozen kit is edited. The enabled consumer records the exact source resolution and uses that declared tolerance. It leaves core_clock_band.band_policy, clock periods/uncertainty, actual bounds, measured data and all stop/resume decisions unchanged. No environment may claim a larger tolerance than the source contract. This proposal does not change a binding or fabricate a typed parent constraint.
