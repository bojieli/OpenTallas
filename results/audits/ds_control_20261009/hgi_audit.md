## Explicit implementation limits and timing scope

- **EHASH B:** shipped constants/type validation, selected-layer hash and ACCEPT history restore are partial DS machinery. Arbitrary B serialization, tokenizer/compression binding and pinned simulator vectors remain provider work (hbm-sim/Claude + ehash). No dynamic-B capability is claimed. Actual O U32 table publication and ACCEPT event producer must be bound; no host fixture becomes runtime wiring.
- **QDQ:** FP8/FP4-UE8M0 block32 and E4 block16 are tested; other E4 param[7:0] block sizes are unimplemented. Normative wording does not enumerate a legal subset. Owner/simulator must pin that subset or lowering; this audit grants neither. Full header param25 and operand/address validation remain dispatcher obligations; engine-local param16/low8 interfaces do not prove safe full-record lowering.
- **Collective:** groups1/2/4/8/96 supported;16/32/64 must be rejected, not silently remapped. Owner-block B1..255 is proposed by new formatter, with DS B8 one-cycle fast map and arbitrary-B41cycle fallback. New full-payload gate must finish before citing it. Decoder +1dispatch edge; formatter up to3extra DS control cycles/row and41-cycle generic mapping must remain in composed token model, not hidden under old zero-cycle endpoint claim.
- **TOPK:** actual2048 compare/shift cells and174080storage bits are sized, but mapped fit/synthesis is pending. m*(n+k+3) is a modeled engine bound, not measured whole-token latency. No existing DS SELECT timing credit transfers to the new engine.
- **G12:** metadata ordering preserves B-before-C; it does not prove KV byte layout, dot/softmax accumulation order, global maximum or payload reader bandwidth. Count correction requires fresh current-source exact/mutant and physical source pins; preserve previous failures/jobs.
- **Physical/composition:** proposed slots/channels and two -cx variants are pathfinding until source-matched measured fit, real clock/IO/credit budgets, electrical checks and routed verdicts arrive. This audit supplies no route/adoption/sign-off verdict. Simulator arithmetic outputs alone cannot establish transaction/fence/completion timing or ±2% stage calibration.
