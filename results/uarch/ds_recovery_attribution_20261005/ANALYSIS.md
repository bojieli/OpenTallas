Temporal layer/expert nonreuse explains resident area and idle occupancy. It does **not** prove latency causality: the measured field phases already exceed their active read/issue floors, with exact recurrence, split-phase serialization, drain/return, VM and wire included. Idle pairs cannot compute another tensor without a valid mapping and schedule. HBM reuses its arithmetic between tensors. Its explicit expert first-access charge is only **5.2124 µs**; all explicit HBM terms total **10.757 µs**, including other traffic. Neither is the total removable weight-stream cost: ingest pacing may sit inside SM service. Keep overlap and first access separate.

Equal-total-area accounting must sum logic dies, every DRAM layer, base/controller dies and switches for **both** ROM KV-HBM and HBM weights+KV. The old ledger totals **801,068 mm² ROM** versus **457,264 mm² HBM** (1.752×); logic alone is 9.448×. Its 1089 mm²/stack is an eight-core-plus-base footprint **bound**, not measured silicon. The adopted recovery draft adds **52 dies / 43,640.4 mm²**, giving a bounded known-inventory sum **844,708.4 mm²** before unresolved inventory changes. Current complete area is unknown. On one layer die, actual weight-ROM bodies occupy **76.2625 mm²** and configuration storage abstracts **40.463 mm²**. q/BF abstracts combine storage and compute; hub/service/network also mix functions. Pure compute/network area is unresolved. Packaging, interposer, yield and cost are separate from raw die silicon.

For batches, **T_aggregate(B)=B/T_batch** and **E_token=P_system/T_aggregate**; MTP counts actual accepted tokens. Ideal dense weight bytes/token are W/B; routed bytes are Σ(W_e for B_e>0)/B, with ΣB_e=6B. For equal-sized experts, union U lies between 6 and min(128,6B). Under the explicitly conditional independent-uniform selection model, E[U]=128[1−(1−6/128)^B]: at B=8/32/128, expected routed bytes/token relative to batch one are **0.850/0.523/0.166**. These are arithmetic reuse bounds, not measured batch throughput or energy. ROM user pipelines can raise active fraction and amortize static power; they do not automatically reuse each ROM read B times. HBM reuse can also reduce energy and become compute-bound; KV grows per user.

The JSON gives conditional resource/energy bounds and the missing current phase-II/controller calendar, occupancy, union selections, KV capacity, read/issue traces, clocks, complete silicon and integrated system-power contracts. With fixed static power, a measured g-fold throughput increase divides static J/token by g; total energy need not decrease when active power grows. Only a best feasible throughput envelope with smaller-batch fallback is monotone; arbitrary actual selection/schedule throughput is not.
