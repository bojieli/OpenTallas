Actual encoding and storage
The command envelope is512bits/64B, matching Dewey. Required scalar fields
include native opcode, PC, family/template/microstep, logical scalar index,
three source version/generation identities (including a predicate), physical
RF addresses, active lanes, rank/SM, destination generation/version and bounded
fragment sequence. Source attributes/rounding and shapes are fetched by pinned
program/template/microstep identity, not silently omitted or charged as free.
One256-bit source digest and program selector are retained per rank. External
literal/attribute/provider metadata is explicitly excluded from the fixed-word
dictionary estimate and remains a charged immutable-provider fetch boundary.

Source-lifetime minima are7 resident versions/SM for Qwen and14 for DeepSeek.
The adopted envelope remains512 entries of at least200bits perSM. Smaller
8/16-entry tables are NOT adopted; they do not prove all dynamic compiler-frame
or provider metadata occupancy.32 temporary RF tags, one command and response,
a64B fetch window, loop/predicate state and scratch lease/address are counted.
There are104 DS declared version references without resident RF homes. They
remain provider-owned state and require typed owner/lease/visibility receipts;
no fabricated RF address or zero-byte/free capacity is allocated.

RF is256KiB logical /512KiB mirrored,512 vectors x128lanes x32bits; scratch is
64KiB with64B beats. Matrix ingest remains a separate128B port. RF payload and
mirrored-write storage are existing H1 resources and are not charged twice.
Three-input operations require an extra serialized RF read/predicate capture;
2R1W does not become a three-read-port RF by lowering SELECT or SCATTER.
