# Measured lattice/channel fill: ROOT review required

Problem: generation accepted a coordinator-reviewed `lattice_channel_bound` sizing reason, but post-route `gate_layout` recognized only `pin_bound`. Merely copying the reason into the post-route check would accept a declared band even when the actual cells were elsewhere.

This change shares the geometry/density check between generation and the gate, and adds a source-bound actual-cell/row census to `layout_dump.tcl`. The gate uses the latest placed checkpoint and records `measured_band_fill` in its receipt. It refuses missing floorplan/placed evidence, stale area statistics, illegal/unplaced cells, off-lattice/overlapping macros, inadequate channels, incomplete full-width legal rows, and cells outside the band. Generation uses synthesis area; qualification uses the actual post-place census. No halo or macro-adjusted effective area is credited as standard-cell mass.

The existing `pin_bound` policy is unchanged for specs that do not declare a lattice/channel reason. Once a spec declares the structural reason, a pin-bound string cannot rescue an invalid claim. The source-binding tool inventory includes the validator module. Actual supplied macros need 43.2um origins, >=20um core-edge and intermacro channels, no overlap with the declared band, and at least four macros. Standard-cell area divided by full core-width band area must be 40–60%.

The reason needs nonempty `why` and `approved_by` exactly `codex coordinator` or legacy `ROOT`. These labels do not replace ROOT's visual review. A new optional `placed_exceptions` list is intentionally strict: exact instance name, role `clock` or `pin`, nonempty reason, and the same coordinator approval. Wildcards, duplicate or unused names fail. An exception must still occupy actual legal rows inside the physical core and avoid macros; its measured area is reported separately and never contributes to band density. No candidate in this packet requests such an exception.
