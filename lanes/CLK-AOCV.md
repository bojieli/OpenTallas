Problem: SS..FF is the full global +-3 sigma spread (lot-to-lot, wafer-to-wafer and die-to-die), not a within-die measurement, so 0.42 %/mm is "all global variation as a radial parabola, edge die". Literature:
  (a) Pang & Nikolic, IEEE JSSC 44(5) 2009, 90 nm: D2D 3sigma/med 15 % "over half a wafer" with a radial pattern; WID 3sigma/mu only ~3.5 %; a sharp 1 % reticle step.
  (b) Cheng et al., IEEE TCAD 2011, Fig. 1: 65 nm 300 mm wafers, RO frequency 7.3-9.4 (~25 % centre->edge). My read of the figure gives a parabola slope of ~0.3 %/mm at the edge, ~0 at the centre, ~0.15 %/mm mid-radius (UNCHECKED figure read).
  (c) Maragos et al., FPL 2019, 16 nm FinFET Zynq XCZU7EV: 7.3 % total intra-die (systematic + stochastic), 8.3 % inter-die (10/12 % across V/T).
  I found no published FinFET within-die gradient in %/mm. The bound x 19 mm = 7.9 % differential across the die root, which is at or above the whole measured 16 nm intra-die spread, random part included. Verdict: 0.42 is defensible only as an edge-die worst case. The evidence supports ~0.15-0.3 %/mm (g/2 = 0.21 sits in that range) as a literature-anchored value. OWNER choice; no rule change proposed.
