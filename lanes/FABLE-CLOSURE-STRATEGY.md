## 0. Headline

1. **Post-place timing produces false stops on every die (R, FABLE-PD-QC-0840).** With ideal clocks OpenSTA subtracts the landing macro's `min_clock_tree_path` (95 to 162 ps) from the capture edge of a port-born `fclk_in_*` clock: the same bus reads -112.9 at place and +39 to +44 post-hook. Reset recovery on the unbuffered reset net is counted as setup: six place stops of -56,163 to -359,026 ps all start at a reset relay, and the same DB reads +551 recovery after CTS. slw_st_a_v2 clf4365a62 crossed the -300 stop by 1.73 ps on the artifact alone.
2. **Global route mis-models two things, and both are now explained (R).** (a) GRT counts tracks next to an obstruction that DRT cannot use: the M2-M7 spacing table gives 0.072 um next to a shape wider than 0.033, so a track 38 to 42 nm from a reserved strip is free for GRT and illegal for DRT (DRT-0255). hd_host_e with the first fix routed to the end with DRT-0255 gone. (b) FastRoute rips NDR clock nets out of edges with an NDR deficit even below capacity: hroot `fclk` 432 um against 55 um routed alone, t9 spread 39 ps on a 3-iteration GRT and 510 ps after 30.
3. **The design failures left share one cause: a row or site keyed to a base that moved (R, I).** KV: `landing_raw_branches` carries literal relay net names on 51 of 56 types, 17 EMIT errors on D27d. S81: the leaf re-grid stacked on J7 puts 6,342 of 25,092 re-sited station registers inside moved cores and 1,181 last hops over 405 um (max 1,032); `u_cf` MX in v3 changes `orient` only, so a 70-bit leg goes to 427 to 480 um on 12 types; duplicate local-reset names failed the J7b bind. KV taps: rows for hub_C, pc_v3 and hg_v3 are solved on placements of superseded netlists. QC: R8 strap registers and `inst_taps` sit where an older placement had their sinks.
4. **First-hop seats at hard-core pins and port registers are the dominant real setup class (R).** QC st_a strap register to core pin -169 on 43 endpoints, sle_ctl -440.7 and -598.7; KV pv vport 715 bits at 476 to 1,345 um (-541 to -561 on v7, and a 275k half repeats it); S81 col_h10_r0_p1 22 groups over 405 um (reset 736 um), lepe_mu_LP `f_host` 600 to 700 um (-292 post-hook); HEAD 9 reset hops of 451 to 572 um (already relayed to <= 286 um half-hop in provider_p6r).
5. **Two results are functional and block views, not timing (R).** enc_j6a post-route LEC is NOT EQUIVALENT on 24 of 6,889 points. The HEAD START transport bench fails 78 of 329, 53 of them on the old provider too, and it has never passed on any applied HEAD plan; the bench was vacuous until 11-21 04:36.
