## Purpose and completion boundary

Produce a reproducible architecture research result showing that the specified models execute correctly, that their hardware can fit and meet its clock under ASAP7 models, and that the decoding comparison follows from calibrated implementation costs. This is a research implementation, not a manufacturing-ready chip. ASAP7 is a predictive research PDK; successful implementation supports feasibility under its models, not commercial-process manufacturability. See the [original ASAP7 publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mejo.2016.04.006).

The active tracks are Qwen3-8B ROM, DeepSeek-V4.1 ROM, and GPU-organised HBM comparators for both models. All four design/model combinations need their own completion evidence. Minimum single-user decode latency is primary; independent-request batching throughput is secondary and must not delay or degrade the single-user path. Keep the current compute-beside-ROM organisation and the established GPU organisation for HBM. More ROM dies are permitted only after pricing their complete single-user latency. The die outline stays within the owner's 26 by 33 mm constraint.

Research scope excludes production DFT/ATE development, foundry qualification, manufacturing yield characterization, tapeout delivery and final commercial package sign-off. It does not exclude finite memory service, routing, clock distribution, power-delivery feasibility, or packaging assumptions that determine the claimed performance. Hardware emulation is an optional acceleration resource, not a required deliverable.

No current product-rate headline is adopted by this revision. Historical figures retain their original evidence scope. N5 storage-density and cost projections remain separate from ASAP7 physical evidence; unimplemented memory density, repair/ECC, package, PHY and cooling assumptions require disclosed bounds and sensitivity analysis.

| Gate | Required evidence | Passing scope |
|---|---|---|
| G0: execution and resource contract | Pinned checkpoint/configuration; complete emitted program; legal storage homes and addresses; source-owned producers/consumers; finite buffers, ports, tags, credits, write visibility and lifetimes; all compute, communication and synchronization costs represented in the unified model. | A chosen implementable configuration. Capacity arithmetic or an ISA graph alone cannot pass. |
| G1: full-program functional correctness | Full-token golden/ISA differential runs for each design/model combination, including routing, selection, collectives, state updates and head; declared arithmetic and reduction order; representative input/context coverage. | Complete functional execution, without RTL timing or physical credit. |
| G2: connected RTL correctness | Actual full-shape subsystem interfaces; dependencies, backpressure, memory responses, reset/drain and clock crossings; then full-token connected RTL with numerical and state comparisons. Directed uniform fixtures cannot establish general token correctness. |
| G3: physical feasibility | One complete full-goal element/SM and irregular service/hub blocks; real memory abstracts and timing, source-matched HBM shoreline controller/PHY boundary; source-matched parent integration, die fit, routing and clock analysis; contextual SS setup and FF hold; workload-based power/IR feasibility. | Feasibility under the stated PDK and macro models. Outline fit, global-route overflow or proxy timing alone cannot pass. |
| G4: calibrated token performance | Same-program model/RTL cycle reconciliation; measured stalls, memory service and collective timing; physically derived crossings; source-matched frequency; reproducible critical-path breakdown and discrepancy resolution. | Calibrated simulated decoding latency, not measured silicon performance. |
| G5: comparative and reproducibility package | Optimized baseline, common workload/precision/context and resource basis; single-user and secondary batching results; ablations; assumption sensitivities; immutable evidence, tool versions, commands and a fresh-run recipe. | A research comparison whose advantages and limits are explicitly supported. |

All gates bind the same configuration identity: program/checkpoint, source revision, generated images, lane and replica counts, memory geometry, clocks, timing constraints, placement and service parameters. Historical or reduced results are useful prerequisites but do not silently qualify a successor. Missing costs remain unknown and block their dependent claim; they are never zero-filled.

## Verification hierarchy and efficient execution

Use the cheapest appropriate layer to expose each problem, while retaining the final connected-token and physical gates:

1. Functional/ISA execution covers complete programs and broad input/context sweeps.
2. The architectural calendar predicts each dependency region and the whole token, including finite service and contention. Give each RTL run an expected cycle budget and source-derived progress predicates before launch.
3. Block tests, formal/equivalence checks and deliberately broken variants validate arithmetic and control checks. Verify that synthesis retains the intended circuitry.
4. Focused connected RTL exercises actual producer-to-consumer paths at full required geometry. Use source-bound reduced control cases for diagnosis, without promoting their scale or rate.
5. Connected full-shape layers cover ordinary, indexed, expert, collective and head paths; connected full-token runs retain persistent DUT state and actual stage-hop interfaces.
6. Broader performance sweeps use the calibrated model. Do not repeat full RTL merely to regenerate an unchanged result or a source-pin annotation.

A timeout is incomplete/failed evidence, not a deadlock diagnosis. A runtime PASS marker needs a qualified checker and raw return receipt. A deliberately broken variant is detected only when its observed assertion/behavior meets the declared negative-test contract; an unexpected exit or parser rejection is not automatically a hardware failure or a passed mutant check.

Hierarchical physical implementation follows model, floorplan, one hardened full-goal element, then replication. Actual SRAM/ROM pins, OBS, halos, PG/clock reservations, multiplexers, fanout, repair and cross-block paths are charged. Die assembly uses real abstracts and contextual clock insertion. Global-route feasibility is preliminary; complete representative detailed routes and parent/die extracted timing are required to support physical claims. No flattened transistor-level simulation of every repeated element is required.

HBM physical acceptance explicitly includes the shoreline controller/PHY assembly, separately for Qwen and DeepSeek resident inventories. Bind lossless system-to-selected-channel address decoding (the current candidate uses AW34 to AW31), LEN/BEAT handling, physical tag ownership, read returns and write-visible completion. A candidate service domain at 1 GHz is not adopted as a closed product clock: price its CDC and pipeline latency against the streaming target, then qualify source-matched SS/FF. Charge actual PHY macro pins/OBS, shoreline channels, clock/PDN reservations, extracted routes and workload power/IR. If PHY internals are external or modeled, disclose that boundary and bound its timing, area and bandwidth; a protocol fixture cannot qualify it.

Streaming clock target remains 1.2 GHz; DeepSeek serial-chain target remains 0.9 GHz. Sign-off uses SS setup with 60 ps uncertainty and FF hold with 25 ps uncertainty, including macro SS clock-to-q. These are unchanged acceptance constraints, not closed frequencies. TT is pathfinding evidence only. Any added pipeline cycle is priced before building its successor.

## Fleet and launch policy

Use local, PVE1 and the new large VM for new work, with measured RAM/disk/CPU headroom and pinned clean worktrees. PVE2 and PVE3 are draining: assign no new work, let protected jobs finish naturally, and report when each is actually empty. The retired AGIdock fleet is not reopened. The later owner instructions supersede the older all-host scheduling default.

Parallelize independent targets and prerequisites. Within one target, parallelize model/source audits, numerical checking, physical interface analysis and already admitted jobs. Share one expensive artifact per source/configuration; use passive collection instead of duplicate simulations/watchers. Reserve memory before launch, bound phase/whole runtime and output, and preserve terminal failure receipts. On this local user-service branch CPU quota is not enforced: use verified inherited kernel affinity and never label a systemd CPUQuota property as proof.

Resource review is an internal engineering gate within the owner's authorization, not a request for the owner to reconfirm routine execution. Retire storage only after live-use checks and preservation of unique changes and evidence. Never remove active sources/checkpoints or change fleet permission/configuration to bypass a resource refusal.

## Calibration, comparison and paper deliverables

C1 reconciles each emitted instruction/dependency region with actual issue, completion, write visibility, memory traffic, wait masks and collective timestamps. Report model error and its cause by component, not a single multiplier from a reduced fixture. Recompose the token after resolving material differences. Functional context construction/loading is reported separately from steady decode service; actual cold-start and warm-state policies are disclosed and priced where claimed.

C2 compares against optimized GPU and GPU-organised HBM execution under matched semantics/workloads. Do not assume a universal GPU kernel-launch penalty; include graph/fusion execution and separately measured or bounded synchronization costs. Report single-user latency first, then secondary batching with shared-service contention. Speculative results disclose accepted-token assumptions and drafter/verify cost. Sensitivities cover memory density, context/concurrency, wire/link/collective latency, frequency, package/power/cooling and service bandwidth; unknown PHY behavior must not become a headline advantage.

C3 publishes an architecture/dataflow diagram, an illustrative floorplan clearly labelled by evidence maturity, representative routed layouts, per-token latency/traffic/area/power breakdowns, the matched-config evidence matrix, ablations and limitations, immutable source/tool/input pins, replay commands and a fresh-run recipe. Energy and cost remain modeled estimates unless independently measured; no silicon-performance wording. Figures are regenerated from authoritative records, with the prose census and both untriaged annotations synchronized and `make check-figures` passing.

## Completion audit

The project is complete only when G0–G5 pass for all four design/model combinations and every claimed mode. Retain full-shape exact connected token execution and contextual physical feasibility as mandatory. No smaller fixture, software-only token, area sum, historical clock or clean report may substitute. Any unresolved assumption is either bounded and explicitly disclosed without determining an unsupported claim, or remains a blocker. Publish the calibrated results together after the audit; do not stop merely because a preliminary speedup is attractive.
