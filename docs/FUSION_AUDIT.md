# Fusion audit: dataflow levels 1-5 across the four designs

Stream FA, 2026-10-01. Historical counterfactual record: `results/uarch/fusion_audit.json`, regenerated by `tools/fusion_audit.py` (test: `tests/test_fusion_audit.py`). Its rate, gain and area rows are MODEL ESTIMATES on the checkpoint's geometry, not new product headlines or adoption evidence. The product rows stay W16b's (`tools/uarch_model.py`); this audit supplies candidates for W16b to price behind the 1% gate. The separate W11 run-level record, [fusion_audit_w11_run_level.json](../results/uarch/fusion_audit_w11_run_level.json), contains source-pinned scheduling estimates and explicitly failed historical reduced RTL observations, distinguished below. No old record is rewritten.

## Method

- **V4.1 ROM.** The checkpoint's per-token DAG at S = 41, 1.2 GHz SS, BF16 columns, with the 0.9 GHz serial domain, W11's measured serial build and W18b's shrunk die. The graph is rebuilt exactly as `cons_v41_rom` builds it; the rebuild reproduces the historical product at commit `8b665e92`, 2,736.7 tok/s on the square C_rotate. These rows do not recalibrate the parent's current model.
  - The SU network is W11's C_rotate on W18b's compact hub, in slow cycles a leg: broadcast 7, operand read 18, element write 17, result 7. An unfused op pays 42 and a reduction 32, as W16b prices them.
  - Each candidate is a counterfactual: legs are removed or dependencies rewired, and the full longest path is solved again. Overlap is honoured; nothing is added by hand. AR and the 6-position MTP verify pass are both solved again.
- **Real-program census.** `hdc_replay_v41.ShapeBuilder` at the shipped shape: embed, 40 layers and the head on one die (2,650 SU ops), plus the exact TP-4 layer-0 program with its 12 collectives. The census records which unit wrote every SU operand, which matvec-to-vector patterns dominate, and whether each reduction is local or cross-die.
- **V4.1 HBM.** W19's executed TP-96 program (`results/rtl/w19_hbm_tp96_program_oreduce.json`), priced with W19's own W15b P=48 NVLS formula and W19's node prices.
- **Qwen3-8B ROM.** The calibrated sequencer replay at the product SS point. A folded op is replaced in place by a one-element op that keeps its waits and barriers. The range's low end adds the epilogue's pipeline depth back.
- **Qwen3-8B HBM.** The reference dependency chain, measured against the HBM token's cycles.
- **Exactness classes.**
  - A: the same operations in the same order.
  - B: a different schedule that the golden mirrors exactly. Example: the golden's chunk-8 / RMS_SPLIT tree rebuilt on a stream with a log-depth stack of partials.
  - C: changes rounding, so it needs a contract change and a quality check. **C items are user decisions; levels 6-7 are excluded (AGENTS.md a4f314fb).**

**Level 4 is not a gain over the model.** It is the condition for realising the model. The DAG overlaps independent chains freely. The same token on an in-order stream unit, in the graph's emission order, costs:

| SU issue model | AR µs | AR tok/s | vs. the DAG |
|---|---|---|---|
| the DAG (free overlap) | 352.4 | 2,838 | — |
| pipelined (an op issues once the previous op has issued) | 389.4 | 2,568 | −9.5% |
| blocking (an op waits for the previous op to finish) | 439.4 | 2,276 | −19.8% |

These are emission-order constraints applied to the analytical DAG, not a replay of ISA wait masks. The off-path SU work is 92 µs against 147 µs on the path (ratio 0.63), an analytical overlap budget rather than measured recoverable time. The −9.5% / −19.8% rate exposures cannot be assigned wholesale to within-SU interleaving or to cross-unit waits. `tools/hdc_program_v41.py:schedule` distinguishes own-unit RAW/reducer hazards and chase exceptions from other-unit RAW/WAR/WAW hazards; it does not unconditionally drain on every SU dependence.

### Level 4: W11 run-level evidence and attribution boundary

FA replayed the committed W11 tool at `86e888364a6fdf2ca5d669e68ffcc8d079dad5cb` in a separate clean worktree, using `python3 tools/w11_su_fuse.py --shape shipped --interleave` and the reduced counterpart. Raw outputs, input SHA-256 pins, the reduced checkpoint digest and immutable Git source references are committed in [fusion_audit_w11_run_level.json](../results/uarch/fusion_audit_w11_run_level.json). [fusion_audit_w11_evidence.py](../tools/fusion_audit_w11_evidence.py) checks source blobs, cycle arithmetic and historical issue attribution; `--replay-tree` additionally regenerates both scheduling outputs on that commit. The W11 source calls `interleave()` a **pass-level estimate only**. These figures are therefore supported scheduling statistics, not measured RTL interleaving gains.

| Shape / SU lanes | Schedule | Sum of run makespans, emitted → interleaved (cycles) | Hidden cycles | Hidden / emitted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipped, N=1024 / M=256, position 1,048,575 | unfused | 293,471 → 285,150 | 8,321 | 2.84% |
| shipped, same | KR depth 64 | 271,197 → 267,916 | 3,281 | 1.21% |
| reduced, N=16 / M=8, position 127 | unfused | 341,421 → 331,820 | 9,601 | 2.81% |
| reduced, same | KR depth 64 | 319,147 → 315,466 | 3,681 | 1.15% |

Each shape has 887 consecutive SU runs and 357 adjacent independent pairs (544 multi-op runs shipped, 543 reduced). Any other unit ends a run: nothing moves across that boundary. The estimator uses conservative region RAW/WAR/WAW dependencies, one in-order issue port, MLAT=5 / ALAT=4, a fixed 42-cycle network leg and 5-cycle fused leg. These are the W11 estimator's historical assumptions, distinct from FA's compact 7/18/17/7 legs. The fusion edges are checked at the tool's default sampled positions; the makespan is evaluated at the final position, not at every context. It sums run makespans rather than solving a token critical path, and leaves the terminal network leg in place. Thus 2.8% means hidden SU-run time, not a 2.8% per-user rate gain; 3,281 is the shipped fused estimate, not the reduced RTL result.

**Measured reduced calibration remains separate.** Commit `db6b1c343451b8dda6af4a4250ffd3a1ace3c2bd` preserves `results/rtl/w11_checkpoint_recovery_20261001/{f00,f2517}.fail.json` and their issue traces. Both have `status=fail` despite `single_step.pass=true`; neither is adoption evidence or SS/FF closure. FA verifies these blobs without changing them:

|---|---|---|---|
| f00 | 318,034 | 163,005 | 208,095 |
| f2517 | 376,638 | 233,937 | 242,085 |

For each SU issue, the attributed interval is its cycle minus the **previous issue of any unit**, charged to the current tag. This includes sequencer waits and is not per-region SU busy time. The verifier recomputes the regional sums from the committed `(cycle, pc, unit)` traces; per-region busy counters are absent. The checkpoint records unfused comparison traces as pending. No paired measured gain can be inferred from two fused configurations with different network settings, and reduced synthetic-token calibration does not qualify the shipped shape or the reference token 21946.

**Concrete missing level-4 gate.** A source-pinned die-token comparison must replay real wait masks and unit readiness against a dependence-only schedule on the same program, geometry, clocks and arithmetic, with matched unfused/fused controls. It must distinguish within-SU RAW/chase/class-drain time from cross-unit producer/consumer wait-mask exposure and inherent producer latency, including collective port exclusivity. It must record exact outputs, issue traces and stall/busy counters, then repeat at full shape before composing any per-user gain in W16b. The analytical emission-order exposure is evidence to investigate cross-unit waits, not proof of their recoverable cost. This replaces the earlier unconditional prioritisation of SU-only interleaving over the buffer.

## The staging buffer (the DaVinci FixPipe analogue)

**What the real program needs.** 1,364 of the 2,650 SU ops take an operand straight from a matvec unit. In order of count:

| Op | Count |
|---|---|
| SiLU·up(·w) | 280 |
| expert-sum adds | 240 |
| KV writes | 160 |
| mHC sigmoid mixes | 160 |
| sums of squares | 132 |
| max | 120 |
| scale | 80 |
| RoPE | 48 |
| divide | 40 |
| softplus_sqrt | 40 |

The 240 expert-sum adds (`moe_sum`) are not in the priced graph at all. This is a historical model coverage gap: assuming a w2 epilogue accumulates the experts in id order (class B) requires an exact implementation gate before the estimate can be adopted.

**Reach on the per-token DAG.**

| | Programmable | Fixed-function |
|---|---|---|
| SU ops reached (of 1,702) | 974 (565 on the path) | 707 |
| chain starts removed from the SU network (of 706) | 500 (70.8%) | 368 |
| reductions removed (of 336) | 212 (63.1%) | 168 |

The long tail a fixed function misses is 267 ops (27% of the buffer's reach): the attention exp, den, sink and normalize, softplus_sqrt, the mHC pre/post sigmoid, and the indexer q. The historical candidate uses programmable epilogue lanes. At W11 commit `498dbbf2`, `tools/hdc_isa_v41_fuse.py` explicitly defines the 4-bit `xkr_epi` as 0 = none and 1–15 = indices into a short micro-program store; the functions are loaded store contents, not fixed opcode meanings. Producer-side `xkr` fields are **reserved, no RTL, must be zero**. This encoding is a contract proposal, not implemented delivery or evidence that the buffer closes.

**The root's pitfalls, one by one.**

1. **Tree order.** The golden sums with chunk-8 runs and a padded pairwise tree (`csum`), or with RMS_SPLIT = 8 segments of P = 8 interleaved partials. A tap that receives the stream in index order rebuilds exactly that tree with one sequential accumulator per chunk and a log-depth stack of partials, so it is class B. A streaming sequential accumulator would be class C and is not proposed.
2. **TP placement.** Of the 212 reductions the buffer reaches, 131 are local (matvec or attention output) and 81 are cross-die, after an all-gather: q_norm and kv_norm on every layer, and the compressor norm. In the exact TP-4 layer-0 program these are `L0.attn` sumsq x2 (after COLL) and `hc_post` sumsq (after the wo_b all-reduce). For cross-die reductions the buffer sits after the collective engine. Alternatively the partials ride the gather: at TP-4 each die owns exactly 2 of the 8 RMS_SPLIT segments, so one scalar a die is a subtree of the golden's tree (class B).
3. **Wire.** Only the removed VM read leg (18), the per-op broadcast (7) and the separate reduction pass are counted. Delivery into the lanes still pays the element write (17), or the broadcast (7) for a scalar.
4. **Inherent wait.** The normalised output needs the scalar first. The scale therefore runs as a second pass over the buffer and keeps its issue time; only the first pass rides the producer stream.
5. **Clock crossing.** The buffer can be the 1.2 → 0.9 GHz ratio FIFO. That removes the separate crossing on 281 on-path producer→buffer edges, an upper bound of 1.25 µs AR, not re-solved.
6. **Sizing and area.** The largest vector that has to wait for its scalar is the 4 × 5,120 FP32 mHC residual (80 KB). With double buffering that is 160 KB, five 32 KB SRAM macros (0.06 mm²).
   - Epilogue lanes: 128 light lanes plus 32 SFU lanes is about 1.6 mm². That is +4% of the 38.6 mm² C_rotate hub block, and 4/3 the producer element rate for the clock ratio.
   - Placement: it sits at the VM strip edge where the field trunk and the collective DMA land. No new long wire is needed; delivery reuses the existing write network across the 10.7 mm hub.
   - These are historical sizing estimates. Port bandwidth, replica/fanout cost and the current floorplan slot must be composed in the unified model before building the candidate; the estimate alone does not qualify hardware.
7. **Scalar broadcast H-tree.** The existing 7-cycle broadcast is already at wire reach from the centre (4,890 µm Manhattan, centre to corner, 748 µm a stage). An H-tree rooted at the hub-edge buffer would reach the far corner in 10 stages. No gain. Its routing would be about 32 bits × 156 mm of track, under 2% of the lane array's M5 capacity.

The new evidence record pins the core and DMA source blobs. This assessment identifies gates at those sites without launching another architecture stream or modifying W16b.

| 3: DMA receive stream and field/scan arrivals; SU reduction result/broadcast path | Golden tree arithmetic exists in SU; there is no qualified producer-stream tap plus scalar-to-lane path in the inspected sources | Reconstruct chunk-8 and RMS_SPLIT trees exactly under stalls and TP placement; preserve multiply/add rounding, masked tails and scalar availability. For each gate: keep pinned originals byte-identical and old failures, default the lever off, verify class A/B exactness, pass the hub routing-layer check, and close new hardware at SS setup / FF hold with 60/25 ps uncertainty in context. Adoption additionally requires a measured RTL gain and W16b pricing of at least 1% per-user rate. The tap-only historical +0.7% AR estimate fails that threshold alone; its composition with delivery needs fresh pricing. HBM follow-up remains limited to GPU-real register/TMEM epilogues and supported fused/overlapped collectives. Class-C collective reassociation is excluded from these gates; QC-NAM is separate and retains its old rejection until its user-directed quality/stability rule passes.

## V4.1 HBM comparator (TP-96, GPU-real techniques only)

W19 baseline: AR 495.2 µs and MTP verify pass 994.4 µs. Collectives take 283.7 µs of the AR (586.4 µs of the pass). There are 265 on the path, 6 a layer, and the NVLS fixed latency is 0.78 µs of each gather.

| ID | Candidate | Class | Instances | AR µs saved | MTP µs saved | Precedent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | gather + hc_post + hc_pre + norm sum-of-squares partial (fused AR + residual + RMSNorm) | B* | 80 | 12.4 | 23.9 | TRT-LLM, NVLS |
| H3 | router gather overlapped with the shared expert | A | 40 | 7.2 | 7.2 | CUDA streams |
| H5 | TMEM-style accumulator + register epilogue (SwiGLU·w, z_quant, q RoPE, softplus_sqrt) | A | 164 | 6.1 | 11.8 | Blackwell TMEM, CUTLASS |
| H4 | EP + smaller TP, or TP-48, at equal cost | — | 0 | 0 | 0 | — |
| H2 | merge the expert-intermediate and ffn-out gathers into one K-split all-reduce | **C** | 40 | 29.5 | 8.0 | Megatron row-parallel |

- **H1 is B\* rather than B.** 5,120 / 96 is not an integer. Rank slices must be cut on tree-aligned runs, or each rank sends several subtree partials; otherwise H1 becomes class C.
- **H4 changes nothing.** The 6 collectives a layer are re-sharding points, so a different split does not lower the count.
- **H2 is class C.** The golden accumulates the FP4 w2 blocks sequentially, and a K-split re-associates them.

Together, H1 + H3 + H5 give 25.7 µs AR (+5.5%), owned by W13b and W15b.

## Related work

- **Huawei Ascend / DaVinci.** Liao et al., "Ascend: a Scalable and Unified Architecture for Ubiquitous Deep Neural Network Computing", HPCA 2021; and Liao et al., "DaVinci: A Scalable Architecture for Neural Network Computing", Hot Chips 31 (2019).
  - The Cube writes L0C. On the 910B, FixPipe applies quantise, bias, activation and element-wise work on the way to the Unified Buffer.
  - This is the model for level 2-3's staging buffer. Its fixed function covers 73% of our reach here.
- **Google TPU.** The MXU result FIFO drains into vector registers (Jouppi et al., ISCA 2017 and later generations), which is the analogue of delivery into lane registers.
- **NVIDIA Blackwell.** Tensor memory (TMEM) is an accumulator store between the tensor core and the CUDA cores, with register epilogues. It is the HBM comparator's H5.
- **Groq TSP.** MXM → VXM streams with no memory stop (Abts et al., ISCA 2020). This is the limit case of cut-through and interleaving, statically scheduled: level 4.
- **Tenstorrent.** Circular buffers between the Tensix compute and data-movement cores. They are the producer/consumer staging with credits that W11's KR chase implements.
