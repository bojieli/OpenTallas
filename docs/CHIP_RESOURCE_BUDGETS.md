## Delivery and reduction service

KV service uses the minimum of the source, SDN and staging rates. One token visits pipeline stages in order; tensor shards transfer concurrently. These are service lower bounds. Prefetch overlap and an end-to-end rate require a finite-buffer schedule, and are not credited here.

| Profile | Source B/cycle | Delivered B/cycle | KV service lower bound (µs, 8,192 context) | Qualified TPOT |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| qwen_x4 | 4500 | 4500 | 67.109 | unavailable |
| qwen_x5 | 26112 | 4608 | 262.144 | unavailable |
| qwen_x8 | 13056 | 4608 | 32.768 | unavailable |

A link endpoint is limited to 64 B/cycle by its 512-bit internal port, regardless of its 900 B/cycle PHY class. Eight endpoints provide at most 512 B/cycle aggregate, not 7,200. A 48 KiB payload requires at least 768 cycles on one endpoint before headers, replay or algorithm rounds. Striping must be explicit to use more than one endpoint.

| Format | Weight bytes/pass | Scale bytes/pass | Lane cycles | Port cycles | Reduction service cycles | Minimum pass initiation interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| bf16 | 16384 | 0 | 128 | 128 | 64 | 128 |
| fp8 | 8192 | 1 | 64 | 65 | 64 | 65 |
| mxfp4 | 4096 | 256 | 32 | 34 | 64 | 64 |

Passes contain 64 columns × 128 K elements. The scalar RE8 has seven adders, each with initiation interval one, and reduces one column per cycle. An eight-tile result needs 448 adds, so 64 cycles of service. Tree propagation to the first result is separate; the last result is 63 cycles later. FP8 scale delivery is charged conservatively per pass; sharing a cached scale across passes requires explicit proof. The table assumes N5 accumulator latency one and excludes admission, stalls and final output conversion.

## Execution contract

- Four ranges per operation; same-object ranges merge. A fifth distinct object sets a sticky global wildcard. Check all candidate ranges before reservation, make reservation atomic against younger issue, and keep it live until every write is acknowledged. Scale objects count separately.
- Frontier streaming is disabled. Serial dependencies wait for complete producers. Any future streaming implementation needs ordered final-byte publication and fault behavior proved at the memory boundary.
- INDEX_SCORE uses BF16-resident keys and BF16 queries, group one. AM-E7 and grouped BF16 are disabled because the lane refuses them. The previous Flash index estimate becomes 84 µs for dots plus 13 µs for head combination under the old 201-tile planning point; it is a sensitivity term, not a new chip TPOT.
- New blocked associations and attention amendments require independent vendor-oracle token qualification. The sequential qualification mode and existing oracle are retained.
